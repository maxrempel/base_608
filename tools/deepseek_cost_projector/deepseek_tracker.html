Balance comes from DeepSeek's own balance API and is snapshotted locally every 30 minutes. History before the tracker started came from the Dax expense ledger. The $7 mark is the documented DeepSeek refill threshold. This page shows two views: the linear view covers the last 30 days with real dates on the horizontal axis, and the logarithmic view below zooms from 20 minutes to 30 days with date labels on its ticks. The page refreshes itself every 30 minutes and again whenever you return to the tab.
