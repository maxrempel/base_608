Abduction and contact experiences are frequent, richly documented, and repeatedly examined through hypnotic regression, but their physical basis is doubted by the mainstream science. Terence McKenna and others treated the contact encounters as a psychic rather than a physical phenomena.  Alien hybridization is reported by experieners as the main goal of alien visitation. Consciousness and verifiability
Most abduction experiences carry strong individual memories and even physical evidence, yet very little can be presented to the public, because these events had very few witnesses or none, with very few exceptions. On their own they can be classified as rare manifestations of a psychic phenomena rather than physical events. One the other hand, the alien hybridization program has strong support in every aspect of the research and the evidence, including hypnotic regressions, reports from experincers who visited the craft in a conscious state, documentation leaked from governments and secret contractors, and ancient artifacts as the major purpose of the alien visitation. What we test
Two signals:
1.	Non-parental insertions: DNA in the child absent in both parents. Mendelian inheritance forbids it, and point mutation, recombination, and archaic admixture cannot produce it.
2.	Starseed-enriched markers: variants carried more often by experiencers than by the general population.
We  will also test a candidate variant discovered by Dean Radin and colleagues,  an intronic variant in TNRC18 on chromosome 7 (rs117910193, G to A) that was shown in most controls but in none of self-identified psychics. Radin proposed that centuries of persecution selected against this psychic-associated form. We argue that If alien hybridization restores psychic function, experiencers should carry the psychic-associated form more often than the general population.  
The work has two purposes: to identify traces of alien genetic manipulation, and to discover the functional genes that are modified by aliens and are likely reposnible for restoartion of psychic and telepathic funcitons. Feasibility
The project is two and a half years old. We have collected 65 experiencers, genotyped 32, and sequenced 6, with more consented families waiting for funding. We have established connections in experiencer communities so we can easily recruit many families once funding is availbel. Our pipelines analyze full trios, single-parent pairs, and single  individuals. We developed the mehtods using 600 families from the 1000 Genomes Project and public autism sequencing data. Early results include a sevenfold enrichment (p < 0.05) of de-novo and rare insertions in the 300-700bp range  in one self-reported starseed family over randomly selected public families. Another result is the observation of a directional pattern in de novo mutations, where some families and some functional regions in one generation mutate toward the ancestral sequence and other families and regions mutate away from the ancestral sequences.
