Part Three: Later Questions and Interpretations

The third quarter opens with Speaker 3 finishing a past-life story about a woman who, as a young oracle, had once taught that people should expand, know, and understand themselves because they are the god. That teaching led to severe punishment, so by her teenage years she began repeating what others said and cut off her connection to herself. A regional leader used her to obtain information, but the false information she produced led to horrible decisions and suffering for thousands of residents. Frustrated, she removed the old filter and returned to pure channeling. When the city was about to collapse financially and go to war with neighboring villages, she shared her true insights with the leader, and within a month the town improved dramatically. She became revered as a strong oracle who gave correct, empowering information.

Speaker 2 then asked about her friends Belle and Wences in relation to her galactic path. Speaker 3 said both carry galactic connections from the Pleiades and Sirius, oriented toward understanding oneself, one's place in the environment, and expressing and growing consciousness, all of which supports deep co-creation.

Asked about the highest expression of her husband John's soul mission, Speaker 3 said his greatest expression is continuing to follow excitement. A major life path is integrating deeper intimacy with fellow humans, especially within the family. In many past lifetimes as a masculine being, he experienced war and saw family as secondary, with few lives where nurturing and compassion mattered deeply. This life expands those feminine heart qualities through a masculine frame. Secondary paths include astral projection and visualization, learned through dream states, requiring consistent grounding while connecting to ethereal energy so experiences can be integrated.

When Speaker 2 asked for one thing she did not currently know, Speaker 3 replied that the entities are excited to connect with her and work through question-and-answer because her questions reflect her excitement. He offered two specific things: first, in her connection to hybridized entities, a female Yahyel being shares a child with her, along with genetics from three unknown humans she will likely never meet. Second, as she continues channeling and integrating consciousness for written or entertainment forms, the coming year will bring strong, hard-to-ignore excitement about a topic she initially feels unqualified to write about; he advised following her investigatory energy instead of dismissing it.

Asked about her strongest connection among ascended masters and archangels, Speaker 3 said Raphael is the strongest archangel, while the Christ consciousness or collective is very strong and includes Buddha and Jesus/Yeshua. Buddha energy resonates with her through both an archetypal connection and a past life in which she knew the historical figure behind the Buddha story. She noted that she has many Buddhas around the house.

She then asked about time remaining and was told approximately thirty to thirty-three minutes. She asked about her night terrors and screaming. Speaker 3 explained that during sleep she works on multiple dimensional levels, including co-creation at the template level of physical reality, deliberately experiencing negative states to process traumas too overwhelming for daily life. Sometimes her consciousness does not return to her body quickly enough, producing feelings of being unable to move or separated from herself. The screaming comes from the bodily fear that her physical functions are failing, as if dying. He advised a pre-sleep meditation visualizing the energy body, allowing only about twenty percent of the emotional body into the astral body, then placing mental consciousness into the auric field and letting eighty to ninety percent of the mental body separate, so emotions remain more present upon waking.

Asked whether her physical DNA is used for the hybrid children, Speaker 3 said it is mostly higher-dimensional DNA, which is physical in another dimension. She consents in that state, giving fourth-, fifth-, and sixth-density versions of her human DNA. Asked if she will meet her hybrid children in this incarnation, he said she already has astrally and likely will hundreds or thousands of times, with memory possibly returning. A physical meeting is less probable but possible, likely after low-scale open contact becomes more accepted.

On conscious open contact within one to five years, Speaker 3 said the outcome depends on definition. Mass landings before governments are less probable; individualized co-creations, including CE5 groups and spiritually adept people having physical contact, are more likely. Human acceptance has already opened the window, making 2027, 2028, and 2029 pivotal years.

Finally, returning to what she does not know, Speaker 3 said the female hybrid parent wished to speak. After a pause, Speaker 4 introduced herself as Shyna, a Yahyel who shares a male child with Speaker 2 named Sheena. Shyna said Speaker 2 has already made her life perfect and is the only human aware they exist. She described their energetic connection: they do not use technology or see through her eyes, but they monitor her energy in live time and are drawn to moments of joy and excitement. Shyna said they have communicated before, mostly with her.
