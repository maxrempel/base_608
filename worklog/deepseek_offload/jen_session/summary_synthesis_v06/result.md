Jen Session, July 23, 2026: Detailed Summary

Part One: Opening Context and the First Questions

Rob, the channeler, began with an apology about computer trouble and a lighthearted reference to Mercury retrograde. Jen explained that she had booked the session on impulse after revisiting an old recording in which the entity Ardif suggested she could help humanity’s ascension by sharing her knowledge through fiction. At the time she dismissed the idea, but three years later she had written a young adult trilogy, The Disclosure Day Chronicles, channeling over two hundred pages and centering on the Pleiadians. She pointed out synchronicities: her June 2 publication date was followed days later by Pentagon soft‑disclosure announcements, and Spielberg’s Disclosure Day movie opened on June 12. Rob noted that the same day extraterrestrials were declared real he had participated in a channel panel called “Contact Event.” Jen said she had gathered a set of spontaneous questions and was open to whichever being came forward. Rob offered Treb or Ardif and recommended starting with Treb, who often invites other entities through. He credited Wendy Kennedy for training him to reach trance in about a minute. Treb then greeted Jen, eager to co‑create.

Jen’s first question dealt with past‑life or between‑life agreements shaping her closest relationships. Treb stated that free will always leads the decision system, with the higher self acting like someone pointing a laser for a kitten—showing a direction but leaving the following optional. Linear time, he said, is only a perspective; past selves are just as probable as futures. He then described a connected past life in early‑1600s Egypt, where she was a trader who discovered a large dark‑purple crystal wrapped in copper. Local priests meditated with it and taught her their technique, bringing months of dream states during which she contacted beings she regarded as gods. She eventually abandoned trading, traveled the African coast, and by age forty‑five had taught meditation to many tribes.

Asked about her most active galactic or star‑family connection, Treb named Pleiadian, Sirian, and Arcturian as the three strongest. On what she should release before the October Venus retrograde recalibration, he said that humans in general must dismantle walls around receiving love and feelings of worthiness, and that she specifically should integrate more masculine energy to balance her first and second chakras and, indirectly, the solar plexus, while continuing to trust intuition without judgment.

When Jen inquired about her primary spirit guides, Treb explained that guides differ from human concepts and lack fixed identities because they have accompanied her across many lifetimes. Four are most prominent now: two humanoid fifth‑density Pleiadians from near Electra representing feminine and masculine duality; a tall, slender, blue, hairless humanoid from Octores; and a Sirian aquatic fifth‑density being with a pragmatic mix of logic and emotion. They work mainly through telepathy, intuition, and psychic ability. Jen expressed gratitude.

She asked whether F. Scott Fitzgerald belonged to her soul family circle; Treb confirmed an over‑soul connection, and Jen felt he had supported her writing. Regarding Taylor Wells and Laura Popink, Treb said both are in her greater over‑soul family and have played different roles across lives, currently helping her learn to give and receive energy and to balance heart‑chakra self‑perception.

Jen recalled Ardif’s earlier instruction to write fiction and asked for the highest‑impact path for The Disclosure Day Chronicles. Treb advised her to keep sharing her excitement and to weave the story into media outlets or willing channels, even independently, through short narrations, illustrations, or a brief animated portion, so audiences encounter the characters whimsically. He suggested finding people on social media with whom she could simply share narrations.

Part Two: Developing Themes and Guidance

Treb added that reading aloud even a forty‑five‑second passage can draw the right collaborators closer, and that the very existence of the written material already offers a helpful vibration. To reach the widest possible audience, he recommended engaging without fixed expectations or mental labels, letting excitement rise in the solar plexus, heart, and throat—a sensation he identified as the higher self signaling a correct path.

Jen then asked about her son James, who had seen a ship. Treb estimated a greater than eighty percent likelihood that the craft was linked to the Yaliel beings—human‑gray hybrids—or to related hybrid groups such as the Esasani, Yahya, Ployell, or Goryell. He explained that both James and Jen’s genetic lineage work with these beings in the astral or dream state, where the hybrids study human genetics. The feeling of being “an alien,” Treb said, reflects a soul with roughly eighty to a hundred Earth lifetimes, comparatively new to the human cycle; James resonates more with extra‑dimensional energies than with Earth’s heaviness, so ordinary life often feels strange or uncomfortable.

On hybrid children, Treb stated that Jen has four: three masculine and one feminine, three from the Yael group and one from the earlier Plaoyell group, all connected through co‑creative agreements across time.

When Jen inquired about the Basque region, Treb described the Basque people as a low‑percentage human group whose DNA received an additional “Syrian energy” tweak—one of thousands of genetic adjustments made to humanity over the last two million years. Carriers of even a small percentage of Basque lineage, he claimed, tend to blend technical or scientific inquiry with spiritual development. Regarding relocation, he said that while the region could support a first‑chakra sense of safety, Jen had already done that work. Instead, he recommended following an inner pull toward places that strengthen the heart and throat chakras, such as certain grid lines crossing North America—including the Southwest, Sedona, Mount Shasta, and arcs through the Great Lakes—and stressed that feeling the vibration of the land and its inhabitants is a reliable guide.

Jen asked whether Draconians caused health problems during the making of an audiobook. Treb clarified that authentic Draconian beings seldom interact with individuals. However, because Jen’s oversoul includes Earth‑oriented reptilian entities, some negatively aligned Earth reptilians were curious and, without intending harm, sparked feelings of disconnection or fear. At the same time, a larger group of neutral‑to‑positive reptilians were drawn to her open crown‑chakra energy and actually helped ground the information being channeled.

For a crystal ally supporting spiritual development, Treb first recommended moldavite. When psychic development came up, he suggested wearing moldavite and a Herkimer diamond together on one piece of jewelry, so the amplified energies ground each other.

Jen then requested a past‑incarnation detail for her daughter Josie. Treb described a lifetime around 100 BCE in what is now northern Greece, where the soul was raised in a temple to serve as a channel or oracle. As a child, when her consciousness was less filtered, messages sometimes emerged that did not merely echo the temple’s established gods.

Part Three: Later Questions and Interpretations

Treb continued the story: as a young oracle, she once taught that people should expand, know, and understand themselves because they are the god. A regional leader used her to obtain information, but the false information she produced led to terrible decisions and suffering for thousands of residents. Guilt‑ridden, she removed the self‑imposed filter and returned to pure channeling. When the city faced financial ruin and imminent war with neighboring villages, she shared her genuine insights with the leader; within a month, the town improved dramatically, and she became revered as a powerful oracle who delivered correct, empowering information.
