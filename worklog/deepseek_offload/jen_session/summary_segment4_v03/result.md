Part Four: Final Guidance and Overall Takeaways

In the final segment, the parent entity returned to discuss twelve visitations in which their son spoke directly to the human participant while her body slept but her consciousness stayed awake; she remembered nothing because, the entity said, her body had not retained it. Seeking reconciliation, the entity invited continued contact. Their consciousness, the entity explained, experiences joy, compassion, and passion in one stream, without human sadness or anger, and they have studied human geography, history, and psychology. The participant shared that most humans feel veiled from connection to all-that-is and galactic beings, so 3D reality feels dense and painful; people seek an outside, sometimes vengeful god, live in guilt and fear, and feel separated rather than seeing themselves in others. She admitted incarnation is harder than expected.

For future contact, the entity instructed her to use intentional automatic writing: sit meditatively, write questions, close her eyes, feel their energy in her heart and stomach, remember the love, place her hands on the keyboard, and connect. She returned love, asked that the other children be told she loves them, and promised to become a better communicator.

Treb confirmed the connection was exciting. Asked if she had just been speaking to another parent, Treb said yes: a feminine entity had shared genetics with her and three other human entities to create the masculine child. She felt complete. With about ten minutes left, Treb shared that Capella is actually four stars—two small red, two large yellow-white—and their planet is the third one around the largest red star. She can connect by finding Capella from the Northern Hemisphere, gazing at it, visualizing the binaries, and entering their consciousness through astral projection; she allegedly has two oversoul entities there. Treb described their race: everyone chooses excitement as a job, soul-paired entities do not reproduce sexually but share DNA implanted into a protein egg by specialists, and children are free, telepathic, and raised collectively.

Asked about the "Frank" collective from a Mike Dooley book—eight scientists 3.8 billion light-years away with a dying planet—Treb said they do not use names, and most beings he works with stay within this galaxy because its diversity already suffices. For her son James, Treb recommended Metatron's energy and sacred geometry, especially Mandelbrot and fractal visualizations, to balance Earth energy and eventually reach galactic consciousness. For her husband, he gave a trauma-healing visualization: in meditation, place the most traumatized child self in a left chair, hold hands, and send love through a heart-and-arm circuit, telling that child the hardships were not their fault and taught strength; then place the oldest, wisest self in a right chair and receive guidance; finally connect all three hearts, and when the chairs are empty know that both versions have been integrated. He suggested doing this daily, weekly, or whenever old traumas surface.

Before disconnecting, Treb said her questions revealed unseen parts of their own energy because all are connected, so her growth expanded them too. They exchanged thanks and good nights. Rob then asked how it went; she replied that it was awesome, that she loved Treb, and that she met "a hybrid child or a hybrid..." where the transcript cuts off.

Overall, the session emphasized her joy and sense of completeness, practical instructions for continuing contact through automatic writing and Capella-focused meditation, a detailed Capellan worldview, and concrete guidance for her son and husband. Unresolved issues remain about why visitations leave no memory, how reliable future communication will be, and the meaning of her unfinished statement about meeting a hybrid child.
