# Channeled Session with Treb and Shyna: Past Lives, Hybrid Family, Book Mission, and Healing Practices

## Opening Conversation

The session opened with informal chat. Jen told the channel that she had loved following his adventures in Japan and asked how his kids were. He said they were doing well, that he had enough support to travel, and that life was busy but not badly chaotic. He joked that his computer was giving him problems, which he attributed to Mercury retrograde. Jen explained why she had booked so quickly: whenever the channel was not offering sessions, she would think of profound questions and regret not being able to ask them. She had gone back to an old recording from a session years earlier and was shocked to hear that one of the first things the entity called Ardif had said was that she could help humanity by offering her knowledge in fiction writing. At the time she had not even heard it, because fiction felt completely irrelevant to her. Now, three years later, she had written a fiction book, the first of a trilogy, called *The Disclosure Day Chronicles*, a young adult novel containing the spiritual and galactic material she loves. She said she was not a writer and did not usually read fiction, yet she had channeled at least 200 pages of it. The channel praised this, saying it never surprised him when old session material later proved powerful. He shared his own example of relistening to a chart reading years later and recognizing accurate predictions about work and his relationship with his daughter's mother. Jen said she would send him a beautiful hardcover copy with a gold-foiled flower of the universe on the cover.

Jen noted odd synchronicities around her book's publication: she published on June 2, then the Pentagon files or "soft disclosure" was announced around June 4 or 5, and on June 12 Steven Spielberg's movie *Disclosure Day* came out. The channel added that the same day the government announced that extraterrestrials were real, a channel panel he was on was named "Contact Event." Both felt the universe was moving in the right direction. Jen then said she had prepared random questions, and the channel suggested starting with Treb because Ardif tends to come through whenever she says "whoever is excited." Treb had apparently been known to invite other beings in. She agreed, he drank some water, entered a brief trance, and Treb came through.

## Treb's Opening

Treb greeted Jen warmly, said he was excited to cocreate with her, and invited her questions. Jen asked what past-life or between-life agreements were still shaping her closest relationships. Treb said free will sits in front of the entire decision system; past-life contracts are not forcing anything. He used the analogy of a kitten chasing a laser pointer: the higher self holds the laser and shows where to run, but the kitten decides whether to follow. He also said past lives are not simply events that happened before, because linear time is a perspective; "past" versions of reality are projected and just as probable as the future. The past life most connected to Jen now was in the early 1600s in what is now Egypt, where she lived as a trader. She stumbled across a large dark purple crystal wrapped in copper, did not know its value, and shared it with local priests. They meditated with it, and over many months she had dream states in which she connected with entities she perceived as gods. After about a year and a half, she lost interest in selling and began traveling east and south down the African coast. By age forty-five she had reached southern coastal Africa, met many tribes and leaders, learned about their gods, and taught her meditation and sleeping-state practices to elders and city leaders.

Jen next asked which galactic or star family connection was most active in her energy field. Treb said three were most prominent: Pleiadian, Sirian, and Arcturian, with many others present but less active. She then asked what she needed to release before the Venus retrograde recalibration in October. Treb said this was common to all humans now: she had already worked deeply on how she perceives people and gives love, but the next step was breaking down walls around receiving love from others, particularly feelings of worthiness. He also mentioned integrating more masculine energy into her second chakra to balance the feminine energy she had worked with so well, which would balance the first, second, and third chakras. He encouraged her to continue exploring intuition without self-judgment.

## Spirit Guides, Soul Family, and the Book Mission

Jen asked about her primary spirit guides and their roles. Treb distinguished "spirit guides" from generic guides, saying these beings have been with her across incarnations and therefore do not keep fixed identities. Then he described four larger guides currently strong in her energy: two Pleiadian humanoid fifth-density beings from near the star Electra, representing masculine and feminine, yin and yang energies; a humanoid entity from a system the transcript renders as "Octores," described as tall, slender, blue, and hairless like artistic renderings of such beings; and a fifth-density Sirian aquatic entity with a neutral, pragmatic energy using both logic and emotional responsiveness. These guides work with her telepathic communication, intuition, psychic ability, and background consciousness.

Jen asked whether F. Scott Fitzgerald was part of her soul family circle. Treb said yes, that the energy associated with that name was part of her oversoul group and soul family, and Jen remarked that she felt he had helped with her writing. She then asked about the deeper thread connecting her lifetimes with people she named as Taylor Wells and Laura Popink. Treb said both are in the greater oversoul families and have played different roles in multiple incarnations; in this lifetime they are connected to human interactivity dynamics, energy exchange, and lessons about giving and receiving, particularly around the heart chakra and solar plexus. He did not see one specific dynamic with either person, but rather a broader connection teaching her self-perception and expression.

Jen brought up Ardif's earlier advice about writing fiction and asked the highest-impact path for bringing *The Disclosure Day Chronicles* to its intended audience. Treb recommended continuing to share her excitement, creating small narrations, illustrations, or short animated portions of the story so people get to know the characters in a whimsical, fun way. He suggested using social media to share forty-five-second readings of impactful passages, which would draw cocreators to help push the energy outward. He also said the simple fact that she had completed the book was itself helpful to others, and advised her not to overthink with expectations or questions of why, where, how, and when. Instead, she should let passion and excitement flare in her solar plexus, heart, and throat; that activation, he said, is the higher self knocking on the door.

## James, Hybrid Children, and the Basque Connection

Jen asked about her son James, who had seen a ship in the sky. Treb said the energy was cocreated between Jen and James, and that although he could not confirm the ship was one of "theirs," the probability was over 80 percent that Yahyel entities were involved, even if the ship belonged to someone else. He said the Yahyel, human-gray hybridized beings, are excited to work with humans who have had earlier cocreations or genetic connections. He mentioned several gray hybridized programs—rendered in the transcript as Esasani, Yahya, Ployell, and Goryell—connecting with Jen and her genetic family backward and forward in time. Jen asked how she works with them now. Treb said this happens in the dream state and astral projected state, for her, her son, and many in her genetic lineage. He described the beings as working with genetics from higher-dimensional material inside bodies, and said most ship sightings by her family would likely be connected to one of these hybridized groups.

Jen said James feels like an alien. Treb said that normally he cannot perceive deeply into another entity without permission, but Jen confirmed James had given permission. Treb saw a wide-open energy. He said James means he does not feel comfortable with how Earth and humans operate. Crucially, Treb said this does not make James an old soul; in fact, "old soul" means going back to the very earliest Earth cycles, and humans are the sixth cycle. James is a relatively new soul with only about eighty to one hundred lifetimes on Earth. His consciousness connects more with extra-dimensional energies and higher chakras than with lower chakras, so Earth feels heavy and strange, and his connection to his guides resonates more than ordinary earthly life.

Jen asked whether she has hybrid children beyond her three children on Earth. Treb said yes: she, like most of her family, has at least one hybrid child, and specifically she has four hybridized children—three from the Yahyel group and one from its predecessor group, Plaoyel; three are masculine and one feminine. Jen thanked him.

She then asked about the significance of the Basque region in her soul history or family line. Treb described the Basque as a specific human collective with a very low percentage of the population, similar to other groups with distinct genetic histories. He said human DNA has been changed through hundreds of thousands of iterations over the last two million years, and one of those tweaks added extra "Syrian" energy—likely meaning Sirian—to create the Basque bloodline. Energetically, people with Basque bloodlines are working with both technical/scientific and spiritual aspects of the spiritual journey. Jen asked whether living in the Basque region would benefit her. Treb said such a place mainly supports first-chakra energy like safety and belonging, which she had already worked on well; moving there was not necessary. Instead, she should follow places she feels strongly pulled to, because Earth's grid lines carry different energy themes for different life periods.

Jen then asked which chakra areas would benefit most from a particular geographic area. Treb said her heart and throat chakras had received much work but needed inflow, especially for communicating what she loves. He recommended places with heart-throat or heart-third-eye grid energy. He described generic lines running across North America: horizontally from Southern California through the Southwest, including Sedona and Las Vegas, to southern Alabama; and vertically through Northern California, Mount Shasta, Oregon, and Washington, with other variations, including one arcing from southwest South Dakota through part of North Dakota toward the Great Lakes. He advised feeling the vibration of both the land and the people, who tend to manifest the energy of the area; in heart-throat places one would see compassion and strong verbal sharing, and even a negative or hateful expression would still indicate strong heart-throat energy.
