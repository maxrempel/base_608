Part Two: Later Questions, Guidance, and Conclusions

Speaker 3 first returned to a past-life oracle story: a young female oracle had been severely punished for genuine channeling, suppressed the ability, and began repeating what others said. A regional leader used her false output to make harmful decisions affecting thousands of residents. Her frustration eventually removed the old filter, and when the city faced financial collapse and war, she shared true information for one month; the town changed for the better, and she became a revered oracle.

Asked about Belle and Wences, Speaker 3 said their galactic links to the Pleiades and Sirius are centered on understanding oneself, one’s place, consciousness expression, and cocreation. For John, the highest mission is following excitement and deepening intimacy, especially within family. Many past masculine lifetimes treated family as secondary; this life is meant to nurture compassionate feminine heart qualities through a masculine form. Astral projection and visualization are open to him but need grounding with ethereal energy.
