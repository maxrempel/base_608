Part Two: Developing Themes and Guidance

Speaker 3 opened by advising that reading aloud even a short passage for forty-five seconds can draw the right collaborators closer, and added that simply having written the material is already a helpful vibration for others. To reach the widest possible audience, Speaker 3 suggested engaging without fixed expectations or mental labels, and instead letting excitement rise in the solar plexus, heart and throat, describing that feeling as the higher self signalling a correct path.

When Speaker 2 asked about his son James seeing a ship, Speaker 3 saw their co‑created energy and estimated more than an eighty percent likelihood the craft was linked to the Yaliel beings—human‑gray hybrids—or to one of the related hybrid groups such as the Esasani, Yahya, Ployell or Goryell. Speaker 3 explained that both James and the enquirer’s genetic lineage work with these beings in the astral or dream state, where the hybrids study human genetics. James’s feeling of being “an alien,” Speaker 3 explained, reflects a soul with roughly eighty to a hundred Earth lifetimes, which is relatively new to the human cycle. Because he resonates more with extra‑dimensional energies than with the heaviness of Earth, he often perceives ordinary life as strange or uncomfortable.

On the question of hybrid children, Speaker 3 stated that the enquirer has four: three masculine and one feminine, three from the Yael group and one from the earlier Plaoyell group, all connected through co‑creative agreements across time.

When Speaker 2 inquired about the Basque region, Speaker 3 described the Basque people as a low‑percentage human group whose DNA received an additional “Syrian energy” tweak—one of thousands of genetic adjustments made to humanity over the last two million years. Those carrying even a small percentage of Basque lineage, Speaker 3 asserted, tend to blend technical or scientific inquiry with spiritual development. Regarding relocation, Speaker 3 said that while that region could support the first‑chakra sense of safety, the enquirer had already done that work. Instead, Speaker 3 recommended following the inner pull toward places where heart and throat chakras are strengthened, such as certain grid lines crossing North America—including the Southwest, Sedona, Mount Shasta, and arcs through the Great Lakes—and stressed that feeling the vibration of the land and its inhabitants is a reliable guide.

Speaker 2 asked whether Draconians caused health problems during the making of an audiobook. Speaker 3 clarified that authentic Draconian beings seldom interact with individuals. However, because the enquirer’s oversoul includes Earth‑oriented reptilian entities, some negatively aligned Earth reptilians were curious and, without intending harm, caused feelings of disconnection or fear. Meanwhile, a larger number of neutral‑to‑positive reptilians were also drawn to the open crown‑chakra energy and actually helped ground the information being channeled.

For a crystal ally focused on spiritual development, Speaker 3 first recommended moldavite. When psychic development was mentioned, Speaker 3 suggested wearing moldavite and a Herkimer diamond together on one piece of jewelry to amplify and ground the combined energies.

Finally, Speaker 2 requested a past‑incarnation detail for his daughter Josie. Speaker 3 described a lifetime around 100 BCE in what is now northern Greece, where the soul was raised in a temple to serve as a channel or oracle. As a child, when her consciousness was less filtered, messages sometimes came through that did not merely echo the temple’s established gods.
