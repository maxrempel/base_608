**1. Strongest safe conclusion**  
No validated biological de novo Alu insertion was identified in this cumulative atlas. All 16 primary loose leads and the single strict survivor remain technical screening candidates, as none have been biologically validated and no exact allele-at-locus recurs across families.

**2. What the attrition says technically**  
From ~37,600 accepted AluYa5 copies and ~8.6 million callable positions, only 16 parent-absent leads survive primary filters, with just 10 passing the strand filter. The strict-quality tier further reduces to one lead. This extreme attrition is consistent with a high true-negative rate for the ALU-DeNovo-1 method; the overwhelming majority of child-supported alleles are observed in a parent (32,870/32,886 at primary), indicating that residual parent-absent calls are rare and likely represent technical artifacts (e.g., alignment errors, strand bias, or library complexity) rather than genuine germline events.

**3. Whether any recurrence or hotspot is supported**  
No recurrence or hotspot is supported. No exact locus appears in more than one family. The observed maximum of four primary loose leads on chromosome 1 is not significant (Monte Carlo P=0.24), and family-level heterogeneity is descriptive and potentially confounded by batch/platform effects.

**4. Most valuable next analysis as more families arrive**  
With additional families, the highest priority is to apply the same exact-copy pipeline to aggregate all candidate leads and test whether any specific locus appears in more than one family. If recurrence occurs, subsequent orthogonal validation (e.g., PCR or long-read sequencing) should be performed before any biological claim. Additionally, stratifying candidates by family batch/platform will help distinguish technical artifacts from genuine biological signals.

**5. Three wording traps to avoid**  
- Calling candidates “de novo mutations” or “validated insertions” without explicit biological validation.  
- Interpreting the Monte Carlo P-value as evidence for a hotspot while neglecting that it is not significant and that no locus recurs.  
- Stating that the method “proves absence” of de novo Alu activity; the results only show that no validated events were found in this specific dataset under the given filters.
