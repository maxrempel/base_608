# The Disclosure Day Chronicles: Beyond the Bermuda Triangle – Full Summary

**Author: J. F. Pleasants (2026)**

Set in the Florida Keys, this young-adult novel follows fourteen-year-old Solara Ray Auñón, known as Sola, through an extraordinary few weeks in which she discovers that she is a gridkeeper destined to help raise Earth’s vibration for the long-awaited Disclosure Day – the moment humanity officially learns of its galactic family.

Sola lives with her Cuban father, Rick, on a 29‑foot Phoenix boat in Key Haven. Her mother, Alana, suffers from severe depression and addiction and resides in a long‑term rehab facility; the pain of that fractured relationship haunts Sola, but she finds maternal warmth in her paternal grandmother, Astria, whom she calls Yaya. Astria is a mystic, a QHHT practitioner and a lifelong communicator with spirits, fairies, and extraterrestrial beings. Sola’s best friend, Indigo Blu, is a germ‑phobic, vegan teen with a kind heart who lives with his free‑spirited mother, Rainbow Skye. The two misfits are regularly targeted by bullies, chiefly Drex VanCraven, a foster‑system kid with a criminal father, his sidekick Luther “Led” Elred, and the school’s “queen bee,” Katie Lane. Sola’s striking appearance – sunset‑red hair, blue‑green eyes and deep cacao skin – makes her a permanent outsider, but she is learning to own her uniqueness.

The story ignites during a school assembly presented by retired captain and amateur astronomer Adam MacMillan, known as Cap. Sola and Blu attend an evening stargazing session at the Southernmost Point, where Cap’s powerful Celestron telescope reveals the Pleiades. As Sola gazes at the Seven Sisters, she experiences a telepathic pulse, a whisper of “You,” and the tip of her hair briefly glows gold. Moments later, a silent, icy‑blue light drops from the clouds and disappears behind Wisteria Island. Sola is certain she has seen a UFO. The group – Cap, Sola, Blu, Astria (who has joined them) and Blu’s dog Cosmo – sets out in Cap’s boat to investigate. On the mangrove‑fringed island they encounter a strange woman in oversized clothes and sunglasses who tries to divert them. Minutes later, Sola has the same woman appear again at the harbour, and eventually on the beach, where she is attacked by a possessed swarm of iguanas with glowing amber eyes. The woman disables them with a device and finally introduces herself: she is Lieutenant Vera Ora of the Galactic Federation of Worlds, a Pleiadian from the star system Sola has just been observing, and she needs Sola’s help.

Vera explains that Earth, which the Federation calls the Blue Pearl, is a highly special planet undergoing a vibrational shift from third to fourth density. Eons ago, many star races contributed DNA to create humanity, and the Federation and the Confederation of Planets have quietly guided Earth’s evolution while respecting free will. Now, the quarantine on the Pearl is being lifted, and Disclosure Day is approaching. Sola has been chosen, by her own Higher Self, to be a gridkeeper – a human whose unique energy signature can anchor dormant ley lines, the planet’s energetic nervous system, and thereby stabilise the frequency required for peaceful contact. Vera cannot look Sola in the eye because they are parallel selves, two incarnations of the same oversoul existing simultaneously; direct eye contact would destabilise the timeline. Vera hands Sola a heavy silver box containing a crystalline key: an ankh that must be deposited at specific coordinates.

Before more can be explained, Nagaai warriors – shapeshifting Reptilians serving the Draconian Empire – appear as park rangers on the beach, grab Sola, and vanish. Sola is dragged onto a stinking Draconian ship and subjected to the brutal journey through Orion’s Portal, an interdimensional gateway fiercely controlled by the Ciakahrr Empire and its terrifying master, Nyxar. Vera, in communication with the Federation commander Kai El (also known as Ashtar), arranges a rescue. As the Draconian vessel exits the Portal, it is intercepted by a team led by Lieutenant El‑An, a gentle, 424‑year‑old Pleiadian. Sola is freed and brought to Errahel, the golden planet in the Pleiades, to recover. There she experiences a world of crystalline spires, fragrant breezes, levitating beings, and a compassionate society that ages only to a youthful maturity. El‑An becomes her guide, explaining the mission, the Twelve Laws of the Universe, the nature of dimensions versus densities, and the role of grid lines. On a table in her quarters she finds a framed text of the Twelve Laws – covering Consciousness, Oneness, Vibration, Rhythm, Sovereignty, Creation, Polarity, Space/Time, Resonance, Free Will, Cause and Effect, and Manifestation – which she recognises as a cosmic manual for existence.

Sola is presented to the Council of Light, where Lord Levian formally asks her to accept her role as a gridkeeper. Initially, she panics and refuses, overwhelmed by the pressure and her own insecurities. El‑An then leads her to a quiet dome and places a selenite sphere in her hands. Through a deep meditative journey, Sola relives the trauma of her mother’s addiction, the pain of feeling unloved and guilty, and the loneliness of her childhood. She finally realises that her mother’s struggles are not her fault, and that she must forgive both her mother and herself. In that moment of profound release, her heart opens and her hair glows brilliantly. She returns to Lord Levian and accepts the mission, her fear replaced by fierce love for her family.

Meanwhile, back on Earth, Vera and her boyfriend, Lieutenant Atlas Arzo – a tall Adarian with Reptilian lineage who followed her through the Portal to protect her – have been shadowing Sola’s friends. Cap, Astria and Blu, devastated by Sola’s abduction, receive telepathic reassurance. Under the direction of Vera and Atlas, they prepare to assist Sola upon her return. Returning through Orion’s Portal with El‑An, Sola is reunited with her loved ones. The Pleiadians explain that the Bermuda Triangle harbours an ancient, dormant portal that Sola accidentally awakened during her first transit. The Draconians are now agitating that raw energy, weaponising it into a super‑squall that will destroy the Keys unless the first ley line is anchored immediately.

The mission begins at dawn on Cap’s boat. Vera, unable to interfere directly due to the law of free will, hovers nearby in a cloaked ship. En route, the Draconians mind‑control Cap, turning him into a zombie who tries to take Astria and Blu hostage. The pair escapes by diving into the sea and are rescued by Atlas just as the Nagaai’s stun guns fire. Cap is treated aboard the Pleiadian ship and recovers. With Cap injured, Sola pilots the boat herself, guided by pre‑programmed GPS coordinates and the spiritual support of Astria, who chants light language and summons a giant whale and a pod of dolphins. As they reach the Bermuda Triangle, a violent waterspout – the Draconian storm – threatens to destroy them. Sola drops the ankh into the vortex, reciting the phrase “Guardians of Earth, Ocean, and Sky! Align this crystalline key with the heart of the world! As above, so below. And so it is.” The key settles, a shockwave of green light bursts forth, and the storm instantly dissolves. Sola, however, nearly drowns; the dolphins Jade and Jason carry her to the surface, and Blu performs CPR, saving her life. The first node is anchored, and the group returns to Key West.

In the aftermath, the Pleiadians and the Draconians both recognise the significance of the event. Vera and Atlas, having revealed to Sola that Vera is her parallel self, give Sola a silver locket with an emergency communication function and depart for Errahel, promising to return at the winter solstice for the next phase. Meanwhile, Drex and Led break into Astria’s trailer to steal the Pleiadian Sil’on suits, which are made of an indestructible material that enables telepathy. Drex has come into possession of a cryptic crypto‑key, dropped by a strange old man named Korvus. That old man is in fact Lieutenant Korvus, the second‑in‑command of the Ciakahrr Empire, shapeshifted into a Hemingway‑esque human. Korvus has tracked Sola’s group to the Keys and has discovered that Drex is a fractal of his own oversoul. He gives Drex the key, which awakens a dark, confident energy in the bullied teen. Later, Drex uses the key to locate the suits, but the break‑in is foiled by Atlas, who retrieves the suits moments before Korvus himself appears to protect Drex. The tense stand‑off ends when Vera stuns Korvus with the ship’s Avak’owan, but the Draconian’s interest in Drex is now firmly established.

As Earth returns to its normal rhythms, new characters enter Sola’s life. Cap’s great‑nephew James Foley, a kind, guitar‑playing British teen, arrives in Key West with his younger sister, Louise. Sola is instantly smitten, and her braids glow gold whenever James is near. She and James grow close, sharing walks, crystals, and eventually a rain‑drenched first kiss after James borrows Cap’s truck to bring her home through a storm. Blu, meanwhile, finds himself working on a science project with Led, who is genuinely remorseful about his past behaviour and seems to be changing. The viral video of Blu fighting a crocodile – recorded by Led – has made the pair minor school celebrities, but the attention also attracts darker forces.

The novel ends with a cascade of threats and portents. A menacing black speedboat, seen earlier near the Triangle, now stalks Sola’s waterfront; inside, government agents have detected the massive energy spike from the grid activation and are listening to Sola’s conversations through directional microphones. Sola receives an ominous text from an unknown number: “Be careful who you trust, Sola. Not everything is as it seems.” A delayed image loads: it shows Katie Lane, the school bully, sitting on her bed casually stroking a huge, spiked green iguana whose eyes glow with the same unnatural amber as the possessed reptiles on Wisteria Island. Katie, it seems, has quietly invited the Draconian influence into her own home. Sola also communicates telepathically with Frank, the pelican who accompanied them on the Bermuda Triangle trip; his true name is Iskandar, and he warns her that she is now known.

Back on Errahel, Vera and Atlas learn that the United States government has abducted two of the Yahyel hybrid children – grey‑human hybrids who are the designated ambassadors for Disclosure – and is holding them in an underground facility. The pair must return to the Pearl during the winter solstice to rescue the children and simultaneously guide Sola through the activation of the next ley line. Their mission is far from over, and the stakes have never been higher.

Thus the stage is set for the continuing chronicles: Sola has awakened to her identity as a gridkeeper, anchored the first node, and found love and friendship in the most unexpected places. But the Draconians, a rogue government, and a shifting social web at Coral Sands High School are all converging on her, and Vera is racing back across the galaxy. The Bermuda Triangle was only the beginning; the true test of Sola’s power, her heart, and her trust is just starting.
