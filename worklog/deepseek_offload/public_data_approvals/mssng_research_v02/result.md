In MSSNG autism whole-genome trios and multiplex families, we will test whether autistic probands differ from unaffected relatives in rigorously validated germline de novo point-mutation spectra or high-confidence non-reference insertions. Aim one calls stringent de novo SNVs/indels and tests a predefined ancestral-allele direction statistic, controlling parental age, ancestry, sex, batch, coverage, callable genome, and family. Aim two detects non-reference insertions and unmapped sequence using positive controls, spike-ins where useful, contamination filters, junction reconstruction, and read validation, classifying source without assumptions. Aim three separates germline, mosaic, and artifacts, then compares burden, spectrum, and context without phenotype fishing. Prior 1000 Genomes work found a modest aggregate direction signal, recurrent hotspots disappeared after filtering, and the insertion detector is not yet exact-call validated; therefore this is falsifiable replication and calibration, not confirmation. References: Yuen et al., Nat Neurosci 2017, doi:10.1038/nn.4524; Trost et al., Cell 2022, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2022.10.009; Werling et al., Nat Genet 2018, doi:10.1038/s41588-018-0107-y.
