TITLE: Validating De Novo Mutational Spectra and Non-Reference Insertions in Autism Whole-Genome Trios and Multiplex Families (15 words)

LAY DESCRIPTION: Autism has strong genetic roots, but many questions remain about how new mutations contribute. In this study, we analyze whole-genome sequences from families where one child has autism and from larger families with multiple affected members. We ask whether children with autism differ from their unaffected relatives in the types of spontaneous (de novo) single-letter changes they carry, and in high-confidence insertions of DNA sequences not found in the standard human reference. We use carefully validated methods that remove contamination and verify findings with technical replication. The goal is to test a specific hypothesis in a way that could disprove it, and to calibrate methods for future studies. We do not start with a predetermined conclusion; the results will be interpreted based on rigorous comparison and reproducibility. (127 words)
