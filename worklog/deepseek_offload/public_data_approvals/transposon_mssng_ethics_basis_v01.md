# TRANSPOSON MSSNG research ethics basis v01

Last edited: 2026-08-01 by Codex (GPT-5.6 SOL)

Status: Compliance basis prepared for institutional review. This document is not an Institutional Review Board decision and does not replace one if MSSNG or an applicable authority requires review.

## Proposed determination

The proposed MSSNG project does not require separate TRANSPOSON research ethics approval because it is limited to secondary computational research using existing coded data. TRANSPOSON and its researcher will not obtain identities, interact or intervene with participants, attempt re-identification, or link individual-level MSSNG data to outside databases. All work will remain subject to the MSSNG consent restrictions, Database Access Agreement, Data Access Compliance Office review, and data-security controls.

## Basis

1. The MSSNG United States Database Access Agreement requires Review Board approval or exemption when required by applicable law. Its application expressly permits an explanation when a project does not require Review Board approval.
2. United States Department of Health and Human Services guidance explains that secondary research does not involve human subjects under the Common Rule when investigators do not interact or intervene with living individuals and do not obtain identifiable private information or identifiable biospecimens.
3. MSSNG states that its research data exclude personally identifying information and contractually prohibits re-identification and unapproved individual-level linkage.
4. This project has no participant contact, intervention, return of individual results, recruitment, or access to the identity key.
5. The project nevertheless involves sensitive genomic data. Contractual review, privacy, security, anti-stigmatization, and aggregate disclosure controls therefore remain mandatory even though a separate local Review Board approval is not proposed.

## Conditions

This basis no longer applies if the project gains access to participant identities or an identity key, contacts participants, performs an intervention, adds unapproved external linkage, returns individual findings, materially changes its aims, or becomes subject to a funding or institutional requirement for Review Board oversight. Any such change must be reviewed before work continues.
