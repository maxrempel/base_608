USE OF DATA  
This is a secondary computational analysis of consented, de-identified data. No participant contact and no attempt at re-identification will occur. All use will comply with applicable law, MSSNG policies, and data-use agreements. Specific aims: characterize rare and de novo genetic variants, including structural variants and transposable-element insertions, in autism spectrum disorder; evaluate inheritance patterns using pedigree structure; assess variant burden in probands versus unaffected siblings/relatives. No raw data will be downloaded initially; analyses will be run on secure cloud infrastructure via the MSSNG portal. No data will be shared outside the approved research team.

RESEARCH ETHICS  
The dataset is de-identified and previously consented for research; no re-identification, contact, or return of individual results is intended. This analysis qualifies as non-human-subjects research under applicable U.S. regulations because it involves existing, de-identified data. Stigmatization safeguards include:
- Family-aware aggregate analysis: treat families as units to avoid singling out individuals or lineages.
- Ancestry as covariate, not biological hierarchy: model ancestry/stratification statistically only as a confounder; avoid essentialist or hierarchical interpretations.
- Suppress small cells: suppress counts or results in cells with very small numbers to reduce identifiability.
- Cautious interpretation: frame findings in measured, non-stigmatizing language; avoid overgeneralizing from limited subsets.

REQUESTED DATA  
Approximately 30x whole-genome sequencing data: aligned/read-level data, variant calls, pedigree information, and limited phenotypes strictly necessary. Sample types: probands, parents, and unaffected siblings/relatives. This design enables family-aware analyses (de novo mutation rates, transmission patterns, and rare-variant burden) while minimizing phenotypic scope.
