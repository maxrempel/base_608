# Scientific portfolio brief

## 1. Portfolio conclusion

Across all current projects, detector/technical signals exist in several areas, but no validated biological mutation, depletion, causation, or phenotype association has been established. Aluminum has the strongest pipeline maturity, with an incorrect "transition" association shown to be technically non-robust. OMEGA has a real descriptive detector contrast but fails exact validation and cannot support biological absence. HOMEWARD has completed approved computational phases and its aggregate direction is not falsified, but remains technically unresolved. Blood-type analysis is underpowered and ancestry/platform-confounded. The currently pinned ledger and heartbeat text are stale and must not be used as portfolio truth. Highest-value next moves are exact Y017 father validation, a single verified Aluminum handoff, and a new independent OMEGA truth set.
