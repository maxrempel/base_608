## Objective

Audit `C:\moma` and identify the models, model families, service providers,
makers, and major production tools that materially contributed to the current
MOMA Lesson 1 video pipeline. Produce a concise, credits-ready inventory with
source evidence.

## Scope

- Read the repository’s bird’s-eye maps, current pipeline documentation,
  configuration, workers, and recent Lesson 1 scripts.
- Include image generation, image-to-video, lipsync, speech/voice, music,
  transcription or dialogue processing, editing/assembly, database/storage,
  and orchestration when they materially contributed.
- Explicitly investigate Fish Audio, OpenAI image generation, SiliconFlow/Wan,
  DashScope/VideoRetalk, sync.so, FFmpeg, Cloudflare D1, and any current music
  service or model.
- Distinguish active/default components from optional, special-case, historical,
  or merely documented components.
- Do not reveal secrets, credential contents, private participant information,
  or unrelated project data.

## Deliverable

Write `result.md` with:

1. A compact table: credit category, model/tool, maker/provider, active status,
   role in Lesson 1, and evidence file path plus line number.
2. A short recommended closing-credit wording that does not overclaim model
   authorship or imply endorsement.
3. Any uncertainties or items requiring human confirmation.
4. Five artistic closing-slide background concepts compatible with the warm
   Art Nouveau Anna title system, ranked from strongest to weakest.

Use only repository evidence. Keep the result under 1,800 words.
