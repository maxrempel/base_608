**Valid but limited.** The diagnostic correctly enforces stringent mapping criteria and orientation consistency, and its symmetry between signal and sham avoids ascertainment bias. The exact-payload claim requires both terminal proxies in compatible orientation, which is necessary but not sufficient for exact recovery.

**Exact limitation:** The diagnostic does *not* validate that the flank endpoints themselves are intact—breakpoints falling inside the 800‑bp flanks (rather than between them) will cause mapping failures or false negatives, even when a true variant exists. It therefore assumes a priori that the variant’s breakpoints lie strictly outside the extracted flank windows, an untested condition that can lead to coordinate leakage if the fixed loci are defined from prior knowledge of breakpoint distributions. This must be documented as a scope restriction.
