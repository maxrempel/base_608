### 1. Short Max-friendly summary
The cultured‑20 control genomes carry 143‑224 OMEGA uncommon (Omun) insertions per genome (mean ~188). In contrast, S2 has only 3 Omun and S3 only 1 – rates far below the lowest control (54 Omun/Gb). This dramatic difference suggests that S2/S3 may have very few Omun insertions, but the comparison is confounded because controls are cultured samples while S2/S3 are not (or culture platform differs). The tiny counts (1 or 3) cannot support any claims about insertion topology.

### 2. Safe interpretation boundary
We can state that the observed Omun counts in S2 and S3 are **extremely low** relative to a cultured‑control population (all 20 controls have >140 Omun). However, we cannot conclude that S2/S3 biologically lack Omun insertions, because culture conditions, DNA source, or sequencing platform may affect detection. The finding is **exploratory** and **culture‑confounded**; any claim of depletion or presence requires matched controls.

### 3. What to test next
- **Culture/platform control**: Sequence S2/S3 using the same culture method as the 20‑control panel, or re‑culture the controls under S2/S3 conditions, to isolate biological from technical effects.
- **Larger topology categories**: Extend analysis beyond Omun to *all retained deduplicated autosomal insertion loci* (or *clean loci*) – this broader set may reveal clustering or spread patterns that Omun (1‑3 events) cannot. Such analysis must explicitly name the category (e.g., “all retained deduplicated loci”) and **not conflate it with Omun**.
- **Powered comparison**: Increase sample size of uncultured (or matched) genomes to determine whether the low Omun rate is reproducible and generalizable.

### 4. Caveats applied
No cryptic terms used. No biological absence claimed. Topology not inferred from counts of 1 or 3. Culture/platform confounding is the primary limitation.
