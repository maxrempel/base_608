**Addendum: Exploratory comparison, culture-confounded**

1. **Safe headline:** Observed Omun rate in focal S3 (0.375/Gb) is lower than cultured controls (mean 70.3/Gb). This contrast is exploratory and confounded by culture status.

2. **What is still reportable now:** Per-participant genome-wide Omun rates with uncertainty. Also reportable: dispersion across chromosomes, inter-insertion distances, allele-fraction spectrum, sequence context, and local repeat/mappability features. These can test whether events cluster or follow a scattered pattern.

3. **What is not claimable:** No definitive Starseed enrichment or depletion. Culture may cause or select some indels, and the control cohort is entirely cultured. The observed rate difference cannot be assigned to ancestry or biology until culture confound is resolved.

4. **How to test culture artifact versus scattered genome-wide pattern:** Compare spatial clustering, repeat context, and sequence motifs between focal and cultured controls. If culture artifacts are localized (e.g., certain regions or repeat types), the scattered pattern would persist across diverse genomic contexts—this can be quantified by permutation tests of clustering and by comparing context distributions.

5. **Best next control set:** Uncultured blood-derived matched controls (same ancestry, same processing). These are the strongest follow-up but not a prerequisite for presenting these exploratory, confounded results.
