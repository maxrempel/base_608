## Safe interpretation

This atlas reports screen observations and technical attrition. A zero means
that no candidate was observed under this screen, not that biological mutations
are absent. Candidate recurrence prioritizes technical and biological review;
it is not evidence of causation. No Starseed comparison or significance claim
is part of this build.
