Part One: Opening Context and the First Questions

The channeler opened with a joke about computer trouble and Mercury retrograde. Jen explained that she had booked impulsively after revisiting an old session in which Ardif said she could help humanity by sharing her knowledge through fiction. She dismissed that suggestion at the time, but three years later had written at least two hundred pages of a young adult trilogy, The Disclosure Day Chronicles, centered on Pleiadian themes. She planned to send the channeler a gold-foiled hardcover. Jen also saw synchronicity between her June 2 publication, Pentagon “soft disclosure” news days later, and Spielberg’s Disclosure Day movie opening June 12. The channeler mentioned that a public extraterrestrial announcement had coincided with his participation in a channeling panel called “Contact Event.”

Jen asked whether he simply brings through whichever entity wishes to speak. He offered Treb, Ardif, or another interested being, explaining that Treb often invites others while Ardif usually comes alone. After crediting Wendy Kennedy with helping him enter trance more quickly, he brought through Treb, who greeted Jen and invited her questions.

Jen first asked about agreements made before birth that might shape her closest relationships. Treb said free will stands ahead of any such agreement. He compared the higher self to someone directing a kitten with a laser pointer: it indicates a direction, but a person may choose whether to follow. He also described linear time as a perspective rather than an absolute order.

Treb then related what he presented as a connected past life in early seventeenth-century Egypt. In that account, Jen was a trader who found a dark purple crystal wrapped in copper. Priests taught her to meditate with it, leading to months of dreams in which she perceived beings as gods. She eventually abandoned trading, traveled along the African coast, and taught meditation to many communities by age forty-five.

Asked about her most active galactic or star-family connections, Treb named Pleiadian, Sirian, and Arcturian influences. Regarding what Jen should release before an October Venus-retrograde “recalibration,” he spoke generally about dismantling barriers to receiving love and feeling worthy. For Jen specifically, he recommended integrating more masculine energy to balance the first and second chakras and, indirectly, the solar plexus, while continuing to trust intuition without judging it.

Treb described four principal guides, while cautioning that guides do not have fixed identities in the human sense. Two were fifth-density Pleiadian humanoids associated with feminine and masculine duality; another was a tall blue humanoid from Octores; and the fourth was a pragmatic Sirian aquatic being balancing logic and emotion. He said they support telepathy, intuition, and psychic development mainly in the background.

Jen asked whether F. Scott Fitzgerald belonged to her soul-family circle. Treb described him as an over-soul connection, and Jen said she felt he had helped her writing. Treb likewise placed Taylor Wells and Laura Popink within her broader over-soul family, saying their present relationships concern giving and receiving energy and balancing heart-centered self-perception, without identifying one shared past-life scenario.

Returning to her books, Jen asked how The Disclosure Day Chronicles could have the greatest impact. Treb advised sharing what genuinely excites her and adapting the story for receptive media channels. Possibilities included short narrations, illustrations, animation, or whimsical character introductions, created independently or with collaborators found through social media.

Part Two: Developing Themes and Guidance

Treb advised that reading aloud even a short passage for forty-five seconds could draw suitable collaborators closer, and said that simply writing the material already contributes a helpful vibration. To reach a wide audience, he suggested engaging without fixed expectations or mental labels and following excitement felt through the solar plexus, heart, and throat, which he described as a signal from the higher self.

When Jen asked about her son James seeing a craft, Treb distinguished the sighting itself from the energy he associated with it. He said the craft need not have belonged to the hybrid groups, but estimated more than an eighty-percent probability that Yahyel or related hybrid beings were involved in the co-created energy around the event. He named related groups including Sassani, Yahyel, Pleiadian, and Greys, though the automated transcript renders some names inconsistently. Treb said both James and Jen’s genetic lineage work with such beings in astral or dream states. He interpreted James’s feeling of being “an alien” as the experience of a soul with roughly eighty to one hundred Earth lifetimes, relatively new to the human cycle and more comfortable with extra-dimensional energy than with the heaviness of ordinary Earth life.

On hybrid children, Treb claimed that Jen has four: three masculine and one feminine, with three associated with the Yahyel group and one with another earlier hybrid group. He framed these relationships as co-creative agreements extending across time.

Asked about the Basque region, Treb described Basque people as a small-percentage human group whose DNA received an additional “Sirian energy” adjustment within what he claimed were hundreds of thousands of changes to humanity over the last two million years. He said even a small Basque lineage may combine technical or scientific inquiry with spiritual development. Although the region might support first-chakra feelings of safety, he believed Jen had already done much of that work. He instead advised following an inner pull toward places that strengthen heart and throat expression, referring to supposed energy-grid lines through the Southwest, Sedona, Mount Shasta, and toward the Great Lakes. He recommended attending to the felt character of both the land and its inhabitants.

He instead attributed some fear or disconnection to curious, negatively oriented Earth reptilian beings associated with Jen’s over-soul, while claiming that a larger number of neutral or positive reptilian beings were drawn to her open crown-chakra energy and helped ground the information. These were Treb’s spiritual interpretations, not independently verified causes of illness.

Finally, Jen requested a past-incarnation detail concerning her daughter Josie. Treb described a life around 100 BCE in what is now northern Greece, where the soul was raised in a temple as a channel or oracle. As a child, he said, her awareness was less filtered and her messages sometimes challenged rather than merely repeated the temple’s accepted gods.

Part Three: Later Questions and Interpretations

This phase began with Treb completing a past-life account about a young oracle. According to the story, she was punished for teaching people to understand themselves as divine, then learned to repeat approved religious messages and suppress her own connection. A regional leader relied on her compromised information, with disastrous consequences. She eventually abandoned the filter and resumed what Treb described as authentic channeling. Her advice then helped a financially endangered town avoid conflict and recover, restoring her reputation as an empowering oracle.

Jen asked about friends Belle and Wences in relation to her galactic path. Treb associated both with Pleiadian and Sirian influences concerned with self-understanding, one’s place in the environment, and the expression and growth of consciousness.

Treb framed this as balancing alleged past lives focused on masculine roles, war, and limited nurturing by developing compassion and feminine heart qualities within a masculine life. When Jen asked for something she did not know, Treb said the entities welcomed her questions because they reflected genuine excitement. On hybrid children, Treb said Jen’s contribution was primarily higher-dimensional DNA given with consent in those states. Finally, Treb yielded to a being introduced as Shyna, a Yahyel who said she shared a male child with Jen. Shyna claimed Jen was the only human who knew of their existence and that they monitored her energy without technology, gravitating toward her joy and excitement. She said they had communicated before, primarily with Jen.
