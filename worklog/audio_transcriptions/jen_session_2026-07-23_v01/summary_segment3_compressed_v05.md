Part Three: Later Questions and Interpretations

This phase began with Treb completing a past-life account about a young oracle. According to the story, she was punished for teaching people to understand themselves as divine, then learned to repeat approved religious messages and suppress her own connection. A regional leader relied on her compromised information, with disastrous consequences. She eventually abandoned the filter and resumed what Treb described as authentic channeling. Her advice then helped a financially endangered town avoid conflict and recover, restoring her reputation as an empowering oracle.

Jen asked about friends Belle and Wences in relation to her galactic path. Treb associated both with Pleiadian and Sirian influences concerned with self-understanding, one’s place in the environment, and the expression and growth of consciousness.

For her husband John’s highest soul expression, Treb again emphasized following excitement. He said John’s central path involves deeper human intimacy, especially within the family. Treb framed this as balancing alleged past lives focused on masculine roles, war, and limited nurturing by developing compassion and feminine heart qualities within a masculine life. He also named astral projection and visualization as secondary paths, provided John remains grounded enough to integrate dreamlike experiences.

When Jen asked for something she did not know, Treb said the entities welcomed her questions because they reflected genuine excitement. He then claimed that a female Yahyel being shared a hybrid child with Jen, together with genetic contributions from three unidentified humans. He also predicted that within a year Jen would feel a persistent pull toward a writing or entertainment topic for which she initially felt unqualified, and advised investigating rather than dismissing it.

Asked about ascended masters and archangels, Treb identified Raphael as Jen’s strongest archangel connection. He also described a strong relationship with “Christ consciousness,” including Buddha and Jesus or Yeshua, and said Jen had both an archetypal Buddha connection and an alleged past life involving the historical figure behind the Buddha story.

Jen then asked about her night terrors and screaming. Treb interpreted sleep as multidimensional work in which she processes negative states and traumas too intense for daily awareness. He suggested that delayed return of consciousness to the body produces paralysis and fear of dying. His proposed pre-sleep meditation involved visualizing the energy body, limiting how much emotional energy enters the astral body, and allowing most mental consciousness to rest in the auric field so that emotion remains more present on waking. This was spiritual guidance, not a medical assessment.

On hybrid children, Treb said Jen’s contribution was primarily higher-dimensional DNA given with consent in those states. He claimed she already meets the children astrally, perhaps hundreds or thousands of times, though conscious memory may be absent. He considered physical meeting less likely but possible after limited open contact becomes socially accepted.

Treb described individualized contact, including CE5 groups and spiritually prepared people reporting physical encounters, as more probable than mass public landings. He named 2027 through 2029 as potentially important years, while making the outcome dependent on what “open contact” means.

Finally, Treb yielded to a being introduced as Shyna, a Yahyel who said she shared a male child with Jen. Shyna claimed Jen was the only human who knew of their existence and that they monitored her energy without technology, gravitating toward her joy and excitement. She said they had communicated before, primarily with Jen.
