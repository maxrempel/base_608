## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

### 1. The "splice" bug (v13) - ROOT CAUSE OF LAST-SPOT DESYNC
In v13, the voice was **spliced**: v10's mixed audio up to 948.22s, then voice re-extracted fresh from the original assembly for the last spot. That cut created a shift. **Fix in v14/v15: take the original assembly's audio as ONE unbroken stream.** No cut anywhere in the voice means nothing can drift. Delay it by exactly the title slide length, pad both streams to the full film length before `amix`.

### 2. No trailing loudnorm pass
The second loudness normalization uses a lookahead that truncates ~1.8s of the tail. This killed the closing slide's music. Solution: do the loudnorm and volume adjustments inside a single filter chain, or pad streams before mixing and skip the final loudnorm entirely.

### 3. Intro music carries through first two spots
The trumpets play over the title slide (3.5s) and the first two spots (reels 1-4), then crossfade (~1.2s) into the approved bed at the reel 4/5 boundary. This means the intro music runs from 0s to ~29.90s (body time), where it hands off to the v10 bed.

### 4. Title slide duration settled at 3.5s
Max: "three and a half seconds." Earlier builds had 7s and 8s; those were guesses overriding a clear number. Final: 3.5s exactly.

### 5. Ramp 100% ? 50% straddles the cut
Max approved: 1 second ramp, starting 0.5s before Anna's voice and finishing 0.5s after. So the music is already ducking when speech begins.

### 6. No fade-in, no fade-out on the bookend track
The trumpets piece is already arranged. Open on its first note (trim the file's quiet lead-in - its first ~0.75s sits at ?38 dB). End on its last note (cut the 2.95s of trailing silence).

### 7. Re-rendering prohibition now formalized

Stored as HARD RULE #0d in project memory, also at the top of the `musicunder` skill. The extra small compressed "preview" copy has been banned - deliver the master only.

### 8. Concat by stream copy (slides + body)
