## GOTCHAS

1. **Never use the `/api/storyboard/assign` endpoint** for merged lipsies - it overwrites `jobs.line_hash` and breaks their baked audio. Write `line_current_clip` directly in SQLite instead.

2. **The window two-shot (`sc05_window_twoshot`) has Ishtab on the LEFT, Anna on the RIGHT** - opposite from all other stills. Prompts must flip the L/R descriptions for window beats, or the speaker swap returns.

3. **"Smile" / "grin" / "warm" words in prompts cause wan2.6 to laugh and smile excessively** on serious dialogue - confirmed across multiple jobs (2776, 2785, 2792, 2809). Strip all emotion-positive words. Use "composed," "calm," "formal," "serious."

4. **Hand/arm motion in walking shots:** "motionless" freezes unnatural hand poses; "arms moving" causes robotic periodic flailing. Best result from neutral phrasing ("walking slowly forward, natural gentle motion" - 2811, 2812).

5. **4-turn clips (?4 speakers alternating) are unreliable for speaker assignment** in wan2.6. The speak-order fix ("left speaks first, then right answers...") helped but was not 100%. The proven sc09 win was 2-line merges - keep this in mind for future scenes.

6. **2ND SPINE is currently sparse** because different fire attempts got different synthetic `line_hash` values. Canonicalizing the hashes (one hash per line-combination) would make all takes of a combination group together for star comparison.

7. **The storyboard's arrangement filter** scopes to one beat by default. The "Whole Scene: ON" toggle drops it to show all rows. This was the root cause of Max not seeing the merged view initially.

8. **MOMA was fully down** at one point and had to be restarted via `start_moma.bat`. The worker is a poll loop that runs detached - check `wan26au_worker_pid.txt` for liveness.
