## GOTCHAS

1. **Never use the `/api/storyboard/assign` endpoint for merged lipsies** - it overwrites `jobs.line_hash`, corrupting the synthetic hash that ties the merged audio to the clip. Write `line_current_clip` directly in SQL instead.

2. **The word "smiles" or "grins" in a prompt ruins the clip** - wan2.6 turns it into random bursts of laughter, even on serious topics. Use "composed, calm, formal" instead.

3. **"Left"/"Right" labels alone are ignored by wan2.6** - must describe both characters by appearance + position first, then state speak-order ("the red-haired woman on the left speaks first, the elder on the right answers").

4. **Directing hand/arm movement in walk-and-talk overcorrects** - "still" freezes hands mid-air, "arms moving naturally" makes robotic motion. Neutral phrasing (don't mention hands at all) works best.

5. **The storyboard is scoped to the current arrangement** - if rows seem missing, check that the "Whole Scene: ON" toggle is active, or that `CURRENT_ARR_ID` isn't filtering. The toggle button is next to Refresh.

6. **The worker only writes `'done'`** - it never stamps `'approved'`. Only Max approves. If clips show as `'approved'` unexpectedly, it came through the GUI approve path, not the pipeline.

7. **Merged lipsies have synthetic line-hashes** - they won't appear in the per-line candidate tile pool (those are matched by real line-hash). They show as the spine "slot" pick. This is expected.

8. **The 15s clip cap forces some singles** - lines 8 and 23 are 13-14s monologues; nothing else fits alongside them.

9. **`silence_buffer=1.0`** adds 1s before + after speech. Short chunks (?13s speech) fit fine. The two long monologues don't have room for full 1s pads inside 15s.
