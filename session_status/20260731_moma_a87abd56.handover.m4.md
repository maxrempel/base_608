## GOTCHAS AND DEAD ENDS

### CRITICAL: `adelay` in ffmpeg filter chains is unreliable
**Do not delay the voice with `-filter_complex "adelay=3500|3500"`.** It may silently fail - it did in v15, producing zero error and zero delay. The safe method: build the delayed voice as a real WAV file (concatenate silence + original audio with `ffmpeg -f lavfi -i anullsrc -i original.wav -filter_complex concat`), verify it programmatically, then feed it as a known-good input to the mix.

### CRITICAL: Never splice the voice track
v13 took v10's mixed audio up to 948.22s and swapped to a fresh extract from the original for the tail. That splice caused a desync at the last reel. The voice must be one unbroken stream from the original assembly, delayed once by the title slide length. No cuts.

### CRITICAL: Resolution mismatch on slides
v12-v13 encoded the title slide at 1920?1080 while the body is 1280?720. ffmpeg's concat demuxer joined them without error, but players would have rescaled the lesson mid-stream. Always encode slides to match the body's exact params: `-vf "scale=1280:720:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=1280:720:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2:color=black,setsar=1,format=yuv420p" -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 3.1 -r 30 -video_track_timescale 15360 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 18 -an`.

### CRITICAL: Trailing loudnorm truncates the tail
A final `loudnorm` pass in the mix chain looks ahead and can truncate ~1.8s of audio. The fix from v14: pad both streams to the exact film length with `apad` + `atrim` before `amix`, and do **not** apply loudnorm after the mix.

### The working folder must be outside Nextcloud
Building in the session scratchpad (`AppData\Local\Temp\claude\...`) prevents Nextcloud from syncing gigabytes of throwaway intermediate files to the cloud. v13 accidentally synced 4.5 GB of intermediates before I noticed and deleted them.
