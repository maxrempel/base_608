## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

### 1. A/V Desync Fix (RC1 bug)
**Root cause:** Cut inpoints not on keyframes. With stream-copy cutting (`-c:v copy`), video starts at the nearest prior keyframe but audio starts at the exact inpoint ? variable per-segment offset, accumulating badly in Part 2.
**Fix:** Snap every inpoint DOWN and outpoint UP to real keyframes, verified via ffprobe. Built reusable tool `kf_cut.py` as a scripted guard (per Max's principle: "hard rules must be scripted, not broadcasted").
**Master keyframe grid:** `0.033008 + 0.5*n` seconds (30fps, IDR every 15 frames).

### 2. Intro False Starts (RC1 bug)
Part 1 from 0-179.8s is Max coaching Noeticus. Real interview starts at 180.22s with Noeticus saying "Welcome, everyone. I'm Noeticus..." Everything before is cut. Applied via `INTRO_CUT = ("part1", 0.0, 179.8)` in `build_cutlist.py`.

### 3. Video Jumping/Distortion (RC2 bug)
**Root cause:** Both raw source and KF master have `has_b_frames=2`. Stream-copy cutting an open-GOP B-frame stream causes glitches at every join - frames near cuts reference frames that got removed.
**Fix:** Rebuild masters with closed GOP + zero B-frames (`-bf 0`), IDR keyframe every 0.5s (`-g 15 -keyint_min 15 -force_key_frames "expr:gte(t,n_forced*0.5)"`), `open-gop=0:scenecut=0`. Proven clean on a 95s test clip (max drift 16ms).

### 4. Video Stabilization - ROTATION-SHAKE BUG (CURRENT, UNRESOLVED)
**Three failed attempts, root cause found:**
- **Attempt 1 (vidstab tripod):** Auto-zoom + rotation correction made the picture breathe and warp. Max: "badly damaged, destabilized."
- **Attempt 2 (tripod, fixed zoom, no rotation):** Still twitched every ~1s on both gentle and stronger settings. Max: "super glitchy... twitches every second."
  - **Root cause of twitch:** OBS records with millisecond timestamps (time_base=1/1000). 30fps needs 33.33ms per frame ? timestamps bounce 33/33/34ms, rounding error resets ~once per second. vidstab chokes on this VFR-like jitter.
- **Attempt 3 (tripod, forced CFR 30fps):** Max picked tripod as "perfect" from the proof clip. Used for RC3 and sizzle.
  - **ROTATION BUG DISCOVERED:** Late in the 84-min Part 2, tripod-lock oscillates rotation - clockwise/counter-clockwise twisting. Max: "shaking around its axis... anti-stabilization." Tripod mode pins every frame to frame 1; baseline camera angle drifts over 84 minutes, so vidstab fights harder and oscillates rotation.
  
**Current fix staged (smoothed + maxangle=0):** Smoothed mode follows a moving average (immune to baseline drift). Rotation correction disabled entirely (`maxangle=0`). Proof clips in `samples/stab_fix2/` - `END_smooth_new.mp4` (fix) vs `END_tripod_current.mp4` (bad). The sizzle exposed this because it pulls the "watch the sky" closer from the very end of Part 2.

**Mandatory encode flags for stabilization:**
- Force `fps=30` BEFORE vidstab (the OBS timestamp fix)
- Smoothed mode (NOT tripod)
- `maxangle=0` (disable rotation correction - kills the twist)
- Encode with closed GOP + bf=0 in the SAME pass (cut-ready master, one render)

## GOTCHAS + DEAD ENDS RULED OUT

- **ROTATION SHAKE (active bug):** Tripod-lock causes clockwise/counter-clockwise twisting late in long clips. Fix = smoothed mode + `maxangle=0`. Do NOT use tripod mode again.
- **OBS timestamp jitter:** Source has `time_base=1/1000` - millisecond timestamps can't express 30fps cleanly. Always force `fps=30` before vidstab or it twitches every ~1 second.
- **B-frame stream-copy glitches:** Any master for stream-copy cutting MUST have closed GOP, zero B-frames, IDR every 0.5s. Otherwise every join glitches.
- **Keyframe snapping:** Inpoints must snap DOWN to keyframes, outpoints UP. `kf_cut.py` enforces this and checks sync.
- **vidstab tripod + long recordings:** Tripod mode anchors to frame 1. Over 84 minutes, baseline camera angle drifts enough that vidstab oscillates rotation trying to pin back. Smoothed mode avoids this.
- **Side-by-side comparison lies:** Stacking a processed clip next to an unprocessed one and re-timestamping for the player can make only one panel stutter - not a real stabilization artifact. Independent standalone clips are the honest test.
- **Windows path colons in ffmpeg filters:** `C:/...` paths with a colon break ffmpeg's filter parser. Workaround: `cd` to the output directory and use bare filenames.
