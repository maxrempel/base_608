## GOAL (Max's words)
Max wants every Claude Code session that opens Chromium through Playwright to have Bitwarden installed and logged in as him (maxrempel@icloud.com). ## ROOT CAUSE DISCOVERED
This is the critical finding that 7 previous sessions missed:

**Extension identity mismatch.** Bitwarden was being loaded from an unpacked folder, which Chrome assigns a **different** extension ID (`hcgcgmickjodmmlcbcjmgklhfadjbcec`) than the web-store-installed version (`nngceckbapebfimnlniiiahkandclblb`). Max's login vault (~26-46MB of LevelDB data) was stored under the web-store ID. The folder-loaded extension had its **own** empty storage under the different ID. So the loaded Bitwarden **never saw** his saved login - it was always logged out. Every previous fix cloned/copied the empty vault thinking it was the logged-in one.

1. **Had Max log in once** into a fresh golden profile (`C:/claude_base/playwright_profile_golden/`) - this recorded his login under the folder-loaded extension ID (`hcgcgmick...`), which is the identity every session actually uses.

2. **Confirmed the login stuck**: verified the golden profile's `Default/Local Extension Settings/hcgcgmick.../` LevelDB contains his account (Max Rempel, maxrempel@icloud.com, activeUserId set).

3. **Built a lean seed** from the golden profile: `C:/claude_base/playwright_profile_seed/` - strips throwaway cache (~75M of Cache/Code Cache/Service Worker) but keeps the Bitwarden vault. ~46MB total.

4. **Updated the launcher** (`C:/claude_base/tools/playwright_bitwarden/pw_mcp_launch.py`): every session now clones the seed into a unique temp directory under `C:/claude_base/playwright_profile_sessions/`, launches Chromium with `--user-data-dir` pointing to that clone, and cleans up on exit.
