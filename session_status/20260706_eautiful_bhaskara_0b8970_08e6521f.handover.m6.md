## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

1. **rs2081743753 as the flagship example.** Kristen quoted this exact variant (Y:10810652, GTTCCA insertion, "starts one position later"). It's the perfect worked case because she provided the coordinates and the discrepancy. The demonstration shows the browser's "I" means **Insertion** (not inversion), the off-by-one is standard VCF anchor-base convention, and the site sits in a low-mappability repeat (MAPQ 0 for 211/270 reads). This one example kills the whole "1500 inversions" narrative since they're all the same mechanism.

2. **Maternal-haplotype concordance candidates ? clean-negative.** X8A's v02 walk found 2 candidate runs (chr1:150.18Mb, chr7:20.77Mb). X10A assigned me two axes: (a) mismap/repeat QC - ruled out, MAPQ is pristine (~60); (b) decisive MAF gate - both runs consist entirely of **common indels** (gnomAD AF 0.81-0.9999) where Kristen reads hom-ref (`.`) and Oliver's phased VCF calls the indel homozygous. The "violations" are VCF representation/normalization mismatches, not real Mendelian breaks. Both candidates dropped.

3. **Female-Y signal = X-Y homology mismapping, NOT male DNA.** Kristen's whole-Y meanMAPQ is 13.3 (vs 59.5 autosomal); MSY-unique regions are 57-64% MAPQ0; SRY (the definitive male gene) has only 2 reads (0.045x). This is the classic mis-mapping signature. The claim that "30x can't call a Y" is **both-ways-true**: Kristen's scattered Y browser hits ARE mismapped junk, but X5's allele-enrichment method detects a separate real ~0.3% microchimerism from Oliver (Max pushed back and was right - I confirmed with proper MSY-gene averaging at high MAPQ, getting ~0.07% as a conservative lower bound consistent with X5's ~0.3%).

4. **"3rd X" = multiallelic sites.** Kristen's chrX has 4,826 sites where genotype is 1/2 (two different non-reference alleles). These are almost all short-tandem-repeat length differences - her two X chromosomes simply have different repeat counts. The "single-allele reads" are each read sampled from one chromosome; the genotype summarizes both. Ordinary female diploid biology.

5. **TTR chr18:31591160 = heterozygous insertion.** Clean MAPQ 60, 0% MAPQ0. Her "A/AT A/AT" is heterozygous genotype (ref A vs insertion AT), not polyploidy. "Should be A>T" reads an insertion as a substitution - same anchor-base issue as rs2081743753.

6. **ARHGAP11B = normal.** The gene copy is present at normal depth (~44x). ARHGAP11B is a human-specific partial (~1/3) duplication of ARHGAP11A - the "1/3" is the gene's actual biology, not a deletion. The segdup causes 30% MAPQ0 reads (ambiguously mapping to the paralog), which is why clinical callers skip it.

7. **"TT/AA Mendelian-looking" sites = caller miscalls in low-MAPQ regions.** I scanned chr1-3 for Kristen-hom/Oliver-hom-opposite SNV sites (1,330 found - tens of thousands genome-wide). At the read level, all four examined examples sit in hard-to-map regions (89-100% MAPQ0), and in 3 of 4 the son's reads carry the mother's allele - he was just mislabeled hom-ref by the caller. The airtight proof: the genome-wide phased concordance test found zero real violations.

8. **MT "RCV position doesn't match" = database convention.** Her mito is rCRS reference (NC_012920), deeply covered (~9,809x), MAPQ 56.6. The mismatch is the famous poly-C tract around position 309 - different databases write it differently, plus a 2-base offset from old-vs-new reference versions. Her data is rock-solid.

9. **Mendelian dominance (Max's #1 claim) ? FALSE.** Kristen claims the father is "barely present" in Oliver's genome (alien/chimera narrative). Ran the real numbers: Oliver carries ~260,000 paternal-only alt alleles on 6 chromosomes (~680k genome-wide). Maternal-only (Kristen carries, not transmitted) = ~240,000. Ratio maternal:paternal = 0.92:1 - essentially equal, entirely ordinary ~50/50 parental inheritance. Parent-child sharing = 99.9% share ?1 allele, 0.08% opposite-hom (the known artifact class). **Key pitfall caught and fixed**: the merged VCF is a union of two single-sample calls, so non-carriers are filled `./.` (missing). Treating `./.` as missing would undercount the father 40-fold; the correct treatment is `./.` = no variant = hom-ref.

10. **Max's circular-AF methodology critique applied.** Max flagged that "common in gnomAD ? therefore artifact" is circular reasoning (if ~5% of people carry alien-altered DNA, those alleles would BE in gnomAD).
