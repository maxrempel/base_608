- **Why:** Full-text matching was structurally broken - long/high-play songs absorbed every word and greedily matched unrelated segments. IDF weighting and margin gates alone didn't fix it. 3 rounds of failure before converging on first-line anchoring.

### Converged matcher rule (round 8, MINMASS=12)
- OPEN_N = 40 (first ~40 content tokens of segment)
- Score = IDF-weighted recall of opening tokens against canon first-line tokens
- CONFIDENT iff: idf_recall ? 0.50 AND matched_mass ? 12.0 AND (margin ? 0.15 OR idf_recall ? 0.80)
- AMBIGUOUS ("A or B?") iff: idf_recall ? 0.50 AND matched_mass ? 12.0
- UNKNOWN ("?") otherwise
- Tiebreaker: absolute matched IDF mass (more distinctive first-lines win ties)
- **Why MINMASS=12:** At MINMASS=6, false positives dominated (64% precision). At 12, precision hit 88% from hand-checked 50 random non-pilot samples. The main false attractor was "??? ?? ???, ?? ???" (3 ultra-common words, hits recall=1.0 trivially) - the mass floor kills it.
