## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

**1. Song identity = FIRST LINE ONLY. Titles/names are garbage.**
Max: "We don't care much about the official name of the song, but we do care about the first line." Then later: "fuck all names. only first line!!!!" Human timecoders are sloppy; queue.json song names are untrustworthy. A song cluster's tag = its first sung line, never a title.

**2. Clustering/grouping = FULL SUNG TEXT, not first line.**
The first-line sliding-window approach was Max's direct correction - "stupid - it is not the correct strategy." First lines are too transient/noisy to *find* songs; they're the *label* after grouping. Full sung text is robust to ~50% ASR garbage because a whole song's text has redundant signal. All prose is stripped; only sung lyrics count.

**3. No re-transcription needed.**
Sibling b15c proved corpus-wide (ground-truth validated) that the 452 existing transcripts are clean (Latin-intrusion median 0.2%, GT first-line recovery median 93/100). The "garbage" impression was an artifact of title-based grouping, not bad ASR. Sol transcription grind was dropped entirely.

**4. Frequent songs only (3+ performances).**
Cut at 3+ plays. These are trustworthy; the rest are noise. This simplifies the canon and filters out one-offs.

**5. No internet verification.**
Cut from scope. Annotation merging is LLM-only. Unknown authors/composers ? output "unknown" (never blank, never guessed).

**6. Ambiguous annotations ? combined for humans.**
When sources disagree on composer/performer, output "Nikitin or Berkovsky?" - not a forced pick. Humans resolve.

**7. DeepSeek does SEMANTICS only, not timing.**
LLMs can't track timestamps reliably. Boundaries are a separate step (b7's domain - they already "optimized the boundaries" and Max wants them extended to unindexed videos).

**8. The b15 consensus DB is already built** (text-grouped, 17,237 clusters, purity 0.988, V-measure 0.968). It's on origin/master. This is the starting point - it needs trimming to 3+ plays and annotation merging added.

**9. Validation can't trust queue.json names.**
Human labels are sloppy. Validation = eyeballing examples, not scoring against ground-truth names.

**10. Concert structure priors (saved in memory):**
- Saturday: one round, 1 song per performer + guest (~1 hour).
- Sunday: round 1 = two songs each, round 2 = one song each.
- One intro announces the performer ? next 1-2 songs are theirs.
- Prose is the source for performer/composer/poet; after extraction, prose is discarded.
