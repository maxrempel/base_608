## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

### The core bug: quick?tap guard measured the wrong duration
- The typer has a filter (`MIN_AUDIO_S`) meant to discard accidental key?bumps so Whisper does not hallucinate a sentence from a sub?second tap.
- After a recent change that enlarged the **pre?roll cushion** (up to 2?s of audio before the key press is included in every clip), the quick?tap guard was looking at the **total** clip length.
- A quick 0.1?s tap became a 2.1?s clip, sailed past the guard, and Whisper produced a phantom transcription. **This was the only clear logic bug found.**
- **Fix applied:**  
  - `Recorder` now tracks how many samples were seeded as pre?roll before recording starts.  
  - At stop time, it computes **held duration** = (total samples - pre?roll samples) / sample rate.  
  - That held duration is passed into the processing queue and used for both the quick?tap guard and the empty?transcript notification threshold.  
- Verified with a numeric simulation: old guard let a 0.1?s tap through; new guard drops it.
