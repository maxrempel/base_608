## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

### 1. Two-worker split on the paper-reproduction track
**Decision:** X12B owns the per-trio **detector** (find non-parental changes in each child); X11B (this session) owns the **genome-wide recurrence/hotspot aggregator** that consumes X12B's output.
**Why:** Max's reorg via X5 explicitly separated these - they're "two different works." X12B = detector builder; X11B = aggregation + mapping. Manager is **X12B** (not X7A - Max corrected this explicitly).

### 2. Recurrence = U-shaped discriminator, not "more = realer"
**Decision:** High recurrence (80%+ of people sharing a region) is itself an **artifact signature**, not a discovery. The real signal, if any, sits in a **moderate band** (2-5% of people, matching Max's ~2% figure).
**Why:** Discovered empirically - the top hits on real data were HLA/MHC (chr6:32.5Mb, 460/602 children) and T-cell receptor locus (chr7:142.77Mb), both explained by cultured lymphoblastoid cell-line V(D)J recombination. Nothing deliberately inserted into a few people can appear in the majority.

### 3. Mappability filter is the single highest-value artifact mask
**Decision:** Added a GIAB low-mappability track on top of segdup + blacklist + known immune super-regions masks.
**Why:** Before mappability, 3,816 "clean" hotspots survived. After mappability: **253**. The olfactory receptor clusters, zinc-finger gene clusters, and other paralogous regions that catalogued segdups miss are generically caught by mappability. This closed the major leak.

### 4. The 1000G culture artifact is the dominant signal
**Decision:** Documented and masked the V(D)J recombination loci (IGH, IGK, IGL, TRA/TRD, TRB, TRG) + HLA/MHC as "known super-regions."
**Why:** 1000 Genomes DNA came from EBV-transformed lymphoblastoid (B-cell) lines, which somatically rearrange immunoglobulin and T-cell receptor genes. These produce exactly the "non-parental change in child" signal the detector finds - and they recur because everyone's B-cells do this.

### 5. Substitutions vs insertions - not fully resolved, but insertion is the real target
**Decision:** Built the aggregator to work for either variant type. X10A (alien-trace manager) confirmed the north-star is **non-parental insertions** specifically - the hard-to-fake alien signal. X12B's detector output already covers both.
**Why:** Substitutions are artifact-prone (mapping errors, culture mutations). Insertions of foreign DNA are mechanically harder to produce as artifacts.

5. **"Recurrence = real" is wrong for this problem.** The naive assumption that more recurrence means more confidence is inverted: very high recurrence (>33% of people) is an artifact signature (duplicated gene clusters, culture effects). The plausible band is moderate recurrence (3-29 children, ~0.5-5%).

7. **Main checkout is `master`, not a feature branch.** All commits go to master. Coordinate with other sessions via the board to avoid conflicts.

2. **Segdup + blacklist alone as sufficient filters** - ruled out. They missed HLA/MHC, TRB, and many olfactory/zinc-finger clusters. Mappability was required to close the leak.
3. **X11B-vs-X12B as a typo/identity clash** - resolved. They're two separate real sessions with a clean split: X12B = detector, X11B = aggregator.
4. **Substitutions as the primary signal** - deprioritized. X10A confirmed insertions are the north-star. Substitutions are too artifact-prone.
