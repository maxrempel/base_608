## GOTCHAS / DEAD ENDS ALREADY RULED OUT

1. **Sites-only vs genotype VCFs.** The 1000 Genomes folder has two types: `.annotated.vcf.gz` = sites-only (0 samples) and `recalibrated_variants.vcf.gz` = full genotypes. The prototype initially grabbed the wrong one and found zero families. Fixed by selecting `recalibrated_variants` files.

2. **S3 credential gap on EC2.** The genome scan initially ran without AWS credentials (it could *read* public 1000 Genomes with `--no-sign-request`, but couldn't *write* to the results bucket). Results were not syncing. Fixed by copying credentials to the box mid-run.

3. **Chr2 partial upload.** The EC2 box auto-stopped before chr2 finished syncing to S3 (310 of 602 children uploaded). The full chr2 was still on disk. Box was restarted, re-synced all 602 chr2 children, then stopped again.

4. **Mendelian filter skipped initially.** Max caught this. The original pipeline didn't verify that each trio was a genuine biological trio before running NPA detection. Validation was added post-hoc on chr21 and chr22. Result: **all 602 trios are genuine** (rates 0.02-0.10%, zero outliers). Max's ~560 memory was from the older callset where 21 families had incomplete data (missing a parent), not Mendelian failures. No exclusions needed.

5. **"Medialization" and "archaic revival" are the same thing Max asked about.** The NPA type histogram (68% novel-in-neither-parent) was his first question. The archaic-direction histogram (are the non-parental alleles ancestral or derived, on average) is the second question. The former is delivered; the latter is pending on X12F.

6. **Orphan SSH processes on EC2.** During initial prototyping, repeated SSH 255 errors (when the hook blocked launches) caused orphan `aws s3 cp` and detector processes to accumulate, eating CPU and corrupting output. The fix was a disciplined single-launch discipline and using `setsid` for background jobs, plus nuking all related processes before a clean relaunch.

7. **No insertions - substitutions only.** Max's paper uses haplotype substitutions (stretches of SNPs). The insertion lane (INSurVeyor, Kristen's BAMs) is a completely separate track (P1). X12B's P2 lane handles only substitution-based NPAs from VCFs. Clarified on the team board to avoid confusion.
