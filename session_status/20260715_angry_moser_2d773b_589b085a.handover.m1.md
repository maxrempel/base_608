- **Use `/v1/images/edits` with `files=` and `data=` (multipart form), never `json=`.** The /generations endpoint will NOT preserve Anna's face.
- **Vega path difference:** If running on the Vega machine, Nextcloud is at `Nextcloud2`, not `Nextcloud`. Adjust all paths.

The model may default to a seated-presenter look unless specifically told she's on her feet.
