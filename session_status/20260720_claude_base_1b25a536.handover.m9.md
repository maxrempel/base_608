## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

### Vocabulary (locked, in spec)
- **ANCESTRAL** - primate base at a site (Ensembl EPO 6-primate reconstruction).
- **MODERN** - human population-consensus base.
- **LEFT / RIGHT** - the two leftover bases (neither ancestral nor modern).
- **RETURN** - a fresh change lands on the ancestral base (homeward = the signal).
- **STRAY** - a fresh change lands on a non-ancestral, non-consensus base (LEFT or RIGHT).
- **FORWARD** - DEPART + STRAY (anything away from the ancestral).
- Site classes: **ANCHORED** (consensus == ancestral), **DRIFTED** (consensus != ancestral).
- Frequency: **RARE** (<0.1%, includes private), **MEDIUM** (0.1-1%), **HIGH** (>1%).

### Strand decision
Stay on the **reference / Watson forward strand** - no strand collapse (Max: *"we will be much better off if we stay in the consensus assembly strand"*). Catalog literally. Simpler and bug-free.

### De-novo filter evolution
- Originally: loose - child ?6 reads of new allele, both parents 0 reads. No GQ/DP/VAF gates.
- **Now (current, critical):** Max forced a re-think about NPA subtypes. A proper germline het must sit near **allele-balance ~0.5** (binomial test, ~35-65% alt fraction). The old loose filter was inflating the effect. **Phase A just proved the private homeward signal drops from z=+8.4 to z=+1.8 under proper balance.**

### NPA subtype taxonomy (Max's correction, just deployed)
Max caught that different parental configurations need different read requirements and should never share one threshold:
- **C1** - parents aa ? aa, child het (a/x). The gold-standard single de-novo. Test allele balance ~0.5.
- **C2** - one parent het (aa ? ab ? child a/x or b/x). Needs higher parental-depth bar.
- **C3** - parents hom-different (aa ? bb ? child shows a/x or b/x). Must also confirm the expected inherited allele is present (or dropout masquerades as de-novo).
- **C4** - parents aa ? aa, child hom (x/x). "Miracle" class - two identical new alleles, germline-impossible. Almost always a hemizygous deletion or paralog collapse. Needs copy-number/mappability tests, not allele-balance.
- **C5** - parents differ, child hom (x/x). Same structural-artifact class as C4.

**C4 confirmed as structural artifact** - Phase A showed those 11,633 calls have mean depth 5.8 (vs 33 normal) and VAF 0.99, exactly the hemizygous-deletion signature. Correctly excluded.

### Control stack (all passed)
1. Composition-matched null (free-outcome: resample mutant from consensus/CpG spectrum).
2. Trinucleotide-context null (built from ANCHORED sites - non-circular).
3. High-confidence-ancestral subset (sites where all 6 primates agree).
4. Chimp panTro6 ancestral replication.
5. **Gorilla gorGor6 ancestral replication** (z=+12.6 - third independent ancestor).
6. Label-flip negative control (scramble ancestral/derived labels ? signal collapses).
7. Permutation floor (shuffle mutations across families within matched bins ? real family spread beats shuffled spread at p<0.0001).

Plus: read-level spot-check (9/10 homeward sites are clean, genuine de-novo reversions by direct CRAM inspection), forward/progressive artifact rejection (C>A/T>G excess is the 8-oxoguanine/Illumina damage signature), and recurrence/targeting ruled out (97-98% of recurrent sites outside the accessible genome).

### Forward/progressive rejected
The apparent forward signal was (a) oxoG/Illumina damage (C>A, T>G lopsided), plus (b) a known mutation-age gradient (T>C accumulating over time). Once C>A/T>G were removed, the forward signal collapsed. Removing those artifacts *strengthened* the homeward signal (z went +8.4 ? +17.7 on the old measurement). The conclusion: **homeward is the only real directional signal.**

### Family distribution
601 families form **one smooth hump** leaning slightly homeward (~67% of families revert more than expected by chemistry). It's population-wide, **not** a fast ~5% subgroup. The family spread is genuinely real (permutation p<0.0001), not ancestry structure (ANOVA F=0.26, ns), not coverage noise. But individual families **cannot be reliably ranked** - split-half correlation is weak (~0.10 at ~20 mutations per half). Real variation exists; you just can't point to one family and say "that one is a strong reverter."

### Chemistry normalization (Max-chosen)
Use **idea 1** - normalize to ordinary chemistry. Safe because Max's hypothesized push is non-chemical and position-specific, so a chemistry null doesn't launder it out. The published Aggarwala-Voight human de-novo rates are the external reference; X12B also used the dataset's own standing-variant spectrum as a rapid drop-in.

### CpG handling
Simple binary split: change at a CpG site ? CpG bucket; everything else ? non-CpG bucket. Report grid separately for each. No 96-channel model.
