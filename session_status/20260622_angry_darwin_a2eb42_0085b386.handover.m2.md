## DECISIONS MADE + WHY

### 1. Always the last take. No exceptions.
Max's explicit rule: *"Always absolutely always take the last take. I always stop at the best one. The fact that transcription sucks is irrelevant."*
Claude had been using a "cleanest complete take" heuristic, which was **wrong**. This rule is now written into both the **method doc** (`retake_cleaner_v06_tomemex.md`) and **global2.md** so future sessions never repeat the mistake.

### 2. Deepgram nova-3, not Whisper
Whisper silently merges abandoned takes; Deepgram surfaces verbatim transcripts including stumbles and restarts. This is essential for seeing all takes.

### 3. END_PAD = 0.05s (was 0.35s)
v01 had ~0.3s of the next phrase bleeding into each clip. Fixed by adding a separate `END_PAD` parameter to `retake_cleaner.py` (the cut command) and setting it to 0.05s. Front pad kept at 0.3s so word onsets survive.

### 4. Lead-pad safeguard
When concatenating clips from different parts of the recording, a segment's start could bleed the tail of the prior retake (they're adjacent in the source). Added protection to trim a safe margin at clip starts.

### 5. "ensure" vs "make sure"
Max's script said "ensure"; his last take says "make sure." Last-take rule wins - "make sure" stays. Max confirmed he didn't notice the change: *"???? i didn't even notice changing it. Good."*

### 6. The split "80 years" line - acknowledged screw-up
Max's last take of "About eighty years ago, alien hybridization program picked up the pace" has a ~5-second pause mid-sentence. The gap-remover stitched the two halves, making an audible cut. **This needs fixing by using the single continuous take instead.** The fix was mid-edit when the user interrupted.
