Max explicitly demanded:

- No "clean negative" conclusions - only **quantitative distributions and numbers**.
- Two completely separate analyses:
  1. **RARITY** - rare insertions/deletions each person carries compared to the overall population. No inheritance logic.
  2. **NON?PARENTAL EMERGENCE** - something on the child's **maternal** chromosome that the mother does **not** have. Only phased, clean?maternal items; everything unphaseable or paternal is **dumped** (not "maybe", thrown out).
- Everything must be categorized (Alu, LINE, satellite, tandem?repeat, unique, etc.). Alu insertions should be classified by "freshness" (young/consensus vs. old/diverged). Nothing is excluded because it is "just a repeat".
- Population frequency must be checked against multiple resources: gnomAD?SV, T2T?CHM13 (second complete human genome), and the HPRC pangenome.
- Use **the actual reads, not k?mer presence**, to confirm/de?novo a candidate.

### WHAT WE DID (decisions + reasoning)

**1. Insertion RARITY (done)**
- Took Oliver's 1,107 reconstructed payloads (detected from soft?clip junctions, then assembled by iterative fishing).  
- Built a **categoriser** that blasts each payload against real Alu/L1/SVA/satellite reference sequences (25 consensi fetched from Dfam, on asto) and also detects STRs, low?complexity, and copy number (segdup vs. unique).  
- Cross?referenced every category against the population:  
  - gnomAD?SV v4.1 (63k genomes) - real allele frequencies.  
  - T2T?CHM13 - presence/absence in a second complete genome.  
  - HPRC v1.1?mc decomposed pangenome VCF (94 haplotypes, 45 samples) - for the repeat?region insertions that gnomAD?SV cannot read.  
- **Result:** ~50 unique/novel?sequence insertions are absent from both reference genomes; the insertion spectra of child and mother are **shaped identically** (the child's higher raw count is only due to his 80? depth vs. the mother's 30?). The bulk of all insertions (Alu, satellite, tandem?repeat) are common.

**2. Non?parental emergence for insertions (done)**  
- Phased the child's unique/novel insertions using the pre?existing whatshap phased VCF and X8A's `per_block_maternal_side.tsv`.  
- Kept only those that cleanly land on the **maternal** chromosome and the mother **lacks** the insert (soft?clip method, not k?mers).  
- **Result:** no confirmed maternal?de?novo insertion. Every candidate dissolved under a close read?level look:
  - Several were inherited (mother's reads matched, just missed by earlier k?mer checks).
