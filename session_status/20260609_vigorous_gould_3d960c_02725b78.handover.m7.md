## DECISIONS + WHY (the bug fix)
Diagnosis: a b-roll is a silent clip line - it has no per-line mp3. The mixboard player therefore fell into its "no audio" path, which advances to the next line on a **hardcoded 2-second fallback timer**, regardless of the clip's real length. Meanwhile the 8s title video kept playing (looped/muted) underneath. Net effect: the line jumped forward at ~2s, so Max only ever saw the opening sliver / tail of the 8s title.

Fix approach chosen: make a silent clip play **once for its real video duration** and advance only when the video actually ends, instead of on the 2s timer.
