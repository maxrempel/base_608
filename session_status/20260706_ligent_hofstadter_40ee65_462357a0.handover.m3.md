## DECISIONS + WHY

1. **SMS ruled out for Russia**  
   Research (live vendor comparison) showed that as of Aug?2025, Russian carriers block **all A2P SMS** by default, and any message containing a URL is classified as spam. Therefore SMS cannot deliver a Zoom link to Russia. Ukraine works but is spotty and expensive. SMS remains a possible fallback for Israel/Germany/US via **Telnyx** (cheapest, good intl routes), but not for Russia.  

2. **Telegram channel chosen over bot or group**  
   - **Bot** - would require each person to press "Start" once; feasible but adds friction.  
   - **Telegram Group** - not inherently one?way; anyone can post.  
   - **Telegram Channel** - naturally one?way (only admins post, subscribers read). Combined with a **private channel + invite link requiring admin approval**, it gives Max full identity control: every join request waits for his manual approval.  

   This matches Max's verification flow: "person sends voice message ? Max confirms they are real ? Max sends them the invite link ? they tap to request ? Max approves."  

3. **Ugly invite link is a feature, not a bug**  
   Private Telegram channels cannot have a custom username; the invite link is a random hash. Making the channel public to get a nice `t.me/...` address would make it searchable and remove the approval gate-defeating the whole point. A short, friendly shortcut `tamza.com/telega` was created as a compromise.

4. **Facebook Messenger group rejected**  
   Messenger groups do not support message?approval or "link + approve each person"; they let anyone with the link in instantly. Max created a separate **Facebook Group** for his own management; that is considered a parallel project, not part of this session's delivery.

5. **No database needed at this stage**  
   The **channel membership itself is the curated list**. Approving a member into the channel is the irreversible step; Max can remove anyone later. This avoids maintaining a separate DB of phone numbers (which are hard to match to Telegram identities anyway). The only source of truth is the channel's approved?member list.
