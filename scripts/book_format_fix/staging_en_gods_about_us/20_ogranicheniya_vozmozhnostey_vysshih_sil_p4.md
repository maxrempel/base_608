# Gods About Us - THE LIMITS OF THE HIGHER POWERS' CAPABILITIES (Part 4 of 7)

*From the book "Gods About Us", Max Rempel*

Once one of the Greys agreed to give an interview, through a conduit. I trust the truthfulness of this interview, since details of the life of the Greys appeared in it that are known to me as their researcher and little known to the public. It was hardly an invention, because to invent such a thing would require carrying out a great deal of research work.

In his interview, the Grey alien, through a conduit, explained that he was a technical worker engaged in the aliens' ecological program and that he was not involved in the Greys' hybridization program (which, as we know, includes the brief abduction of many people). He had been permitted by his leadership to answer general, educational questions. In particular, from his answers it was clear that despite the fact that the Greys constitute a single mind, it so happens that they can serve on the spaceships of warring cosmic powers. But when the Grey was asked what the purpose of the Greys' hybridization program on earth was, he found it difficult to answer, saying that this

information was inaccessible to him. He was asked how it is, then, that you are part of a common mind and yet cannot answer a simple question. He replied that access to this information was blocked for him, and that he was not supposed to attempt to gain access to the blocked information.

This example is especially interesting in that it allows us to surmise how our communication with the unified human mind might possibly be organized: it will be telepathic and structured. We will know not everything, but only what we need at a given moment. This is precisely how the representatives of the Sassani civilization - Bashar and Adronis - describe their interaction with their common mind. They say that they know nothing until the information is needed, but that when certain information is needed, it appears in their consciousness of its own accord.

A rather convenient system, is it not, and, note, very different from the modern accepted one. In our society it is customary to concentrate on memorizing information, most of which a person never needs in life. After the union into a single mind, people will no longer need to memorize anything. On the contrary, they will need to learn to think! That is, to use access to radically larger volumes of information and to apply it. That is, education that gives knowledge will have to be transformed into the training of ability. Ability - from the word "able." Not to memorize but to be able. Not to memorize but to think. In principle, with the appearance of the Internet, access to information has already been radically eased, and with the spread of smartphones a person has access to this information anywhere, anytime. So the question of transforming education arises already now. Schoolchildren and students must no longer be stuffed with knowledge, but taught to think. This was the position held by Einstein. He said: "Why should I remember what I can look up in a reference book?"

Let us sum up what has been said: how might our transition into the fourth density take place?

1. The development of telepathic abilities will probably continue, first and foremost because the noise will diminish on those waves on which this communication takes place.

2. A gradual union of humanity into a single mind will probably take place. This mind will probably be structured, and individual minds will probably not cease to function. Individual minds will probably communicate with the collective mind as with something external, the way an individual user communicates with the Internet.

3. The development of telepathic abilities will allow a person to communicate not only with other people, but also with other beings, such as: angels, aliens, one's own soul, the souls of other people, animals and plants, and the spirits of the deceased.

4. The lifting of the veil will probably allow a person to remember their past lives - incarnations. This will eliminate the fear of death and people will become wiser. Children, coming into this world, will already remember their past lives, and they will come to be treated as equals.

5. The notion of time will become more blurred. A person will gain the ability to manipulate time within certain limits, will cease to be tied to a single point in time, and on the contrary, will be capable of living simultaneously in today, tomorrow, and yesterday. In addition, they will be able to live in several versions of reality at once. This is hard to understand, but it is precisely to this that a multitude of independent sources testify.

6. Telepathy will bring humanity closer to the galactic community. Union into a single mind will probably bring nations and strata of society closer together and thereby probably contribute to disarmament and integration into the galactic community.

7. To estimate the timeframe for this transformation, I rely first and foremost on the estimates of Bashar, as well as the estimates of other alien sources I trust. It should be especially noted that these forecasts are very approximate and the situation may change simply because individual people and all of humanity have, and retain, freedom of choice (even taking into account that we are being manipulated). The timeframe for the transformation is quite optimistic:

December 2012 - the update and reboot of the planet's software. The formal end of the quarantine on the planet. A turning point in the process of lifting the curtain that separates our consciousness from the spiritual world. A turning point in the development of human telepathic abilities. Thereafter this development will continue for another 20 years, until 2032, when the main transformation will be completed.

2013-2017 - the wider public becomes aware of the fact of the existence of alien civilizations.

2023-2027 - open, official, voluntary contacts with aliens. The establishment of alien diplomatic missions on earth. According to Bashar, the first to communicate with us will be the alien races most closely related to us genetically.

Approximately 2033 - the completion of the transformation into the fourth density. The formal beginning of earth's entry into the galactic community, the beginning of disarmament.

Approximately 2050 - the acceptance of earth into the galactic community.

Let me repeat once more that these forecasts are approximate and based on the predictions of various alien channels.

Thus we have arrived at the culminating point, at the theses, in a sense. We began with research about the soul; I sketched in general terms the sources of information, such as hypnotic regressions, the messages of clairvoyants, contactees, and channeler-conduits. I sketched briefly the veil that separates our mind from the soul, and my understanding of how our collective transition into the fourth density will take place. We touched on such topics as reincarnation and the coming Contact with extraterrestrial civilizations. Of course, in our introductory conversation many questions have remained unanswered.

The information I have outlined may give an unprepared reader a sense of unreality, of fairy-tale, so much does it fail to accord with the generally accepted version of the universe. In the concluding part, I will try to connect the theories expressed with life, to help you accept and integrate the new theory into life.
