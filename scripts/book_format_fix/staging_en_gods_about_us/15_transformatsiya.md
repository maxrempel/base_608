The date of December 21, 2012 is based on an understanding of the precession of the Earth's rotation.

Astronomers know well that the Earth's axis of rotation wobbles slightly, like the axis of any spinning top. Every child knows that a top spins - that is, it dances slightly. This dancing of the axis is called precession. The Earth's axis precesses very slowly: one precessional revolution takes 26,000 years.
