The next important question concerns the vertical energy flows that connect us with God, the source of life.

There are two such flows - the descending and the ascending. The descending flow brings life into inanimate matter and animates it. The ascending flow carries energy and experience upward, which is in essence one and the same thing.

The energy that we supply upward is necessary for the existence of our souls. We are part of a food chain. Its lower part is well familiar to us: plants feed on simple matter, such as water, mineral salts, carbon dioxide, and light; animals feed on plants; we feed on animals. Traditionally the pyramid ends here, placing us at its head. At each level there is an increase in the frequency of vibration; each level rises above the previous one, translating it to a new quality: simple matter - slowly moving plant life - plant-eating animal life full of movement and simplicity - human life filled with drama, technology, information, and reflections on the meaning of life. Further on stands the veil. Beyond the veil is our soul, our higher self, which feeds not on flesh but on feelings, experience, love. Of course, the food chain, or better to say the energy chain, does not end here. Souls are united into a collective mind, which feeds on their experience and energy and solves collective tasks; further on come the angelic entities, contemplating the fate of planets, life on planets, and similar questions of high scale. Higher still is an innumerable quantity of divine levels, concerned with the fates of stars, galaxies, universes, with time, dimensions, and other fundamental concepts that one can only guess at.

These two flows of energy are not an abstraction but knowledge that can be quite usefully applied. The first application is in the healing of oneself and others. Energetic healing is based on the understanding of the flows of energy. There exist several main schools of energetic healing - Reiki, Qigong, yoga, acupuncture, and others.
