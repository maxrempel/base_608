# Gods About Us - USING THE LAW OF MENTAL ATTRACTION

*From the book "Gods About Us", Max Rempel*

To use the law of attraction in life, it is important to distribute energy correctly in time - that is, to pay attention to the temporal particularities of its use.

For example, if you want to become a writer, it is not enough to dream that you will become a writer someday in the distant future. What matters is that right now, in the present moment, you experience the very feelings, the very mood, that a real writer experiences. You must write now, in actual reality. You must meet with readers now, experience now everything that pertains to being a writer.

The example of the mirror is characteristic. For the reflection to smile, it is not enough to want it; you must smile first.
