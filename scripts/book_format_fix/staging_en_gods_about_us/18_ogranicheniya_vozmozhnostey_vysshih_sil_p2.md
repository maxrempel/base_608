The hardest thing for them is to coordinate with us in time. Note that the inhabitants of the 4th, 5th, and 6th densities dwell not only in a different time than we do, but have a notion of time very different from ours. As I mentioned, in the 4th density time is probably three-dimensional, and a person there is not obliged to move through time in one direction at one speed.

Modern films and computer games accustom us to this idea. Imagine a video film in which you are not obliged to move through time continuously forward. You can watch it from any point, at any speed, and even backwards. This is a typical example of free, non-linear movement through time. But time in this example is one-dimensional. To picture a simplified multidimensionality, imagine that an important event - a soccer match, say - is broadcast on many television channels, filmed by different camera operators from different points of view. Switching channels, you move, as it were, between different versions of this event. Now imagine yourself as a chess player going over with a friend a chess game you have played, recording the moves. Now you rewind the game closer to its beginning and change the course of play, playing it anew from a certain point. This is already a true multidimensionality of time: you can move not only back and forth through it, but also into parallel worlds where the game went down a new path. In exactly the same way, in a dream we sometimes manage to avoid trouble by rewinding events and replaying them anew. What for us is only a dream is, for the higher densities, the norm of life. In dreams we visit the higher densities - we are by no means strangers there. This is very important.

It is reported that whales and dolphins are an advanced intelligent civilization dwelling simultaneously in the 3rd and 4th densities. That they were settled on earth with an important purpose - they are the Keepers of the planet, and their task is to maintain the vibrations of the 4th density on our planet and not to let them die out. It is said that they came to us from the vicinity of Sirius, where they were people and walked on land. Life in our oceans allowed them to fulfill their important role without interfering in our affairs. It is said that when we make the transition into the 4th density, they will hand over to us their role as keepers of the planet.

I did not mention dolphins here without reason. It seemed interesting to me to connect the structure of their brain with their ability to dwell simultaneously in the 3rd and 4th densities. It is well known that dolphins are always asleep while awake. That is, they are constantly and simultaneously both awake and asleep. When one hemisphere of a dolphin is asleep, the other is awake, and from time to time the hemispheres switch roles so that each has the chance to sleep.

It may be precisely this ability to sleep while awake that allows dolphins to dwell in two densities at once. We too dwell in the higher densities in our sleep, but an artificial veil prevents us from remembering our life in the higher realms. Dolphins probably do not have this veil.

Here it is worth mentioning that the fourth density, although closer to our notion of paradise, is not paradise. Dolphins are good proof of this - they are far from angels in their treatment of one another; while playing they often nip one another, and in their mode of feeding they are predators.

How will the transition into the fourth density take place on our planet?

This is a mystery. The inhabitants of the higher realms tell of possible scenarios, but explain that the choice remains with us, with our collective consciousness, and on many levels at that.

That is, in our world the path is not predetermined. Our transformation is watched over with great involvement, but by the terms of the game the choice remains with us.

Nevertheless, certain observations allow us to surmise how the transition into the fourth density will take place.

First, we are told that our world is to a significant degree artificial. We have already well grasped that our genome is a digital code and that the human organism is programmed at the level of DNA. It turns out that our invisible support system, our multi-level soul, also has a complex digital code. This applies both to the souls of people and to the souls of all living beings on earth, and, importantly, the soul of our planet also has its own digital code, similar to a genome.

The first and key change leading to our transition into the fourth density consists in the updating of the program that determines the development of the soul of our planet. The messages from above describe in fair detail the structure and updating of the planetary program. It is handled by the inhabitants of the fourth and sixth densities. The ships of fourth-density people carry out work at key points of the planet. Some of these points coincide with the capitals of the world, some with ancient holy places.

This is a fairly well-studied subject, dealt with by researchers who connect astrology with geomagnetic points. The ships of fourth-density people are hidden at a great height in stationary positions over the active points of the planet, and they control the frequencies of the planet's etheric vibrations and the transition from lower frequencies to higher ones.

Thus the experiment on our planet enters a new phase. It is said that on the day of the winter solstice of this year, a reboot of our planet's operating system will take place, and thereafter it will function on updated software.

For now it is difficult to objectively register these changes in the functioning of the planet. The graphs of the Schumann resonances are changing, but not radically enough. Nevertheless, the messages about updates to the planet's operating system come from a multitude of diverse channels from above, and they explain a great deal.

The second sign of the transition into the fourth density is quite noticeable. I have already mentioned that the key sign of fourth-density civilizations is the union of individual minds into a collective mind.
