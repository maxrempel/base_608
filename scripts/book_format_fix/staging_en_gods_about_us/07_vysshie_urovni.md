# Gods About Us - THE HIGHER LEVELS

*From the book "Gods About Us", Max Rempel*

How many of these levels are there?

According to Bashar, their number approaches infinity.

Bashar is the conventional name of a source communicating with us from a parallel world through a conductor (channeler, medium) named Darryl Anka. I am well acquainted with Bashar's messages and regard Bashar as one of the most informative and reliable sources. Bashar has been transmitting for 26 years now, and throughout all those years, beginning in 1986, the information he has supplied has been of very high quality. One of the advantages of the Bashar channel is that he answers questions from the audience. This capacity for two-way communication with the audience is a great rarity among conductors, and therefore the topics that Bashar illuminates are more relevant and intelligible to us.

A site devoted to Bashar: http://bashar.org/ (English)

Video. Bashar speaks about death: http://youtu.be/zouCxJ_tM0w (translated.)

Video. Bashar on the years 2010-2012: http://youtu.be/3oPGcDwd5sM (translated.)

So, the number of levels above the afterlife world approaches infinity. Nevertheless, we communicate mainly with the higher levels nearest to us, and it is they, above all, who are involved in our affairs. Despite the tangled nomenclature of the levels, they are rather easy to remember and understand, since we are already substantially familiar with them. Moreover, they are an important and intimate part of our own constitution, and we are organically connected with them.
