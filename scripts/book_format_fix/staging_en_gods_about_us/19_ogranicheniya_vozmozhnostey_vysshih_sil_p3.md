# Gods About Us - THE LIMITS OF THE HIGHER POWERS' CAPABILITIES (Part 3 of 7)

*From the book "Gods About Us", Max Rempel*

On our planet the first steps toward the union of people into a collective mind have already taken place. The first step in this direction was the use of books for the universalization of education in the age of industrialization; the second step was television, which, for all its shortcomings, radically brought people closer together; and the third step was the development of the Internet and mobile communication. Again, people themselves have changed little, but new connections have appeared and a new type of connecting people together, which brings us closer to union into a single mind. Let us note that in neither the fourth nor the fifth density does the union of individual minds into a single mind eliminate or dissolve individuality. To understand how this can be, consider the Internet: minds are connected without dissolving. In the same way, in alien telepathic civilizations: telepathic communication between people does not greatly diminish their individuality.

What is telepathic communication, and are there signs of the appearance in people of the ability for telepathic communication?

Telepathic communication takes place at the level of the soul; it does not use the electromagnetic waves known to modern science. Telepathy has been fairly well studied. Some pairs of people possess the ability to communicate telepathically. Research shows that neither distance nor the physical shielding of electromagnetic waves has any effect on the telepathic connection. The most characteristic example of telepathy is the ability of some people to guess which of their loved ones is calling. Again, the connection takes place at the level of souls, and between close people this connection is stronger. Counting the guesses of who is calling is not the most impressive in form, but it allows statistics to be gathered, and the statistics unambiguously confirm the non-randomness of the guessing.

There are quite a few independent studies indicating that we are in principle capable of telepathy; however, on average, of course, people's telepathic abilities are rather weak, and it is doubtful that they could be developed by training alone.

The channels of the higher realms report that a human being in principle carries within them a hidden capacity for telepathy, but that this capacity is artificially suppressed. Our inability to be telepathic can to a substantial degree be explained by the presence of an artificial veil that separates us from our souls. The high sources report that at the time when the Anunnaki were creating modern humans, they deliberately spoiled our genome in order to limit our ability to connect with the soul, and that the inability for telepathy in particular was artificially created by jamming the etheric frequencies, the etheric waves on which telepathic communication takes place.

That is, we are told that in order to exercise control over us, certain powers use telepathic jammers that fill with noise the waves on which we might communicate.

If so, then the lifting of the veil will probably be carried out by gradually weakening the jammers and reducing the artificial noise in our etheric ether.

Recently I found on the Internet not proof, but at least a hint, of how the telepathic union of humanity will probably take place.

At the beginning of 2012 I came across this story on a forum devoted to the Bashar channel. I discussed this story with its author and quite trust it. The author recounts that he lives in Australia and works at night. Once he had a hard time waking up after a daytime nap and was lying in a half-doze. In this state he suddenly found himself in telepathic contact with a Gloria from the USA whom he did not know. They had a conversation, and she told him that her telepathic ability had also recently awakened and that for some time now she had been talking, in a half-sleep, with strangers located in different parts of the world. The author of the post recounts that since then he sometimes manages, in a half-sleep, to converse with other strangers.

This story seems to me a very fitting piece of evidence of how the awakening of humanity into the fourth density may take place. Probably many of us will simply discover in ourselves the ability to converse at a distance without the help of technological means. We are already prepared for this by telephones and the Internet. The next step is a natural (formerly supernatural) connection directly.

How this global connectedness of people will give rise to a single mind of humanity is a mystery.

I can surmise how this will take place on the basis of my own experience and the accounts of the inhabitants of the fourth density.

First, my own observations of my own thinking.

From childhood I had the ability to guess the answers to mathematical and technical problems. One might attribute this to intuition, but I believe that intuition too is based on a supernatural appeal to a higher mind. In this case, probably to the collective mind of humanity. In order to obtain the solution to a difficult problem, I act in the usual way: I do half the work - I understand the meaning of the problem and I kindle, I stir up in myself a feeling of curiosity. At the same time I try to maintain inner equilibrium and to treat the problem lightly and cheerfully enough. Because I know that problems treated as difficult do not get solved. Then I touch my forehead with my fingers, close my eyes, and detach myself from the solution, awaiting the answer from above. Often it comes at once; sometimes one has to go after the answer several times and from several sides. It is important to understand that in order to catch the answer, one must tune in to its wavelength, one must be ready to receive it. The answer does not come into a head filled with a thirst to get it. To receive the answer, a question must be posed; there must be a place in the head where it could land, tuned to the wavelength of reception. This is not only my way of appealing to the collective mind. Roughly thus do all people who actively use intuition appeal to it. As you can see, we already use the collective mind; after the removal of the veil we will simply start using it far more actively.

Here it should be noted that telepathy and the collective mind do not mean total openness. As with the appearance of the Internet, many secrets come to the surface and the level of thinking rises, yet the possibility nevertheless remains of keeping secrets and limiting public access to secret information.

Here it would be apt to give an example, as an illustration of the fact that with the transition into the fourth density and the union into a single mind, structuring and secrecy remain, though probably to a lesser degree.

This example concerns the aliens called Greys - that is, the gray ones, or the little gray ones. The Greys dwell in the fourth density, and it is well known that they are united into a single mind. They rarely transmit messages through conduits and, on the whole, their activity is surrounded by secrecy. They secretly abduct and return both Americans and the inhabitants of certain other Western countries and Australia.

In Russia they appear very rarely, far more rarely than other types of aliens.
