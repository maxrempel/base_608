# Welcome to Earth! A Guide for Aliens - EXAMPLES OF SUCCESS

*From "Welcome to Earth! A Guide for Aliens" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

By looking at the main countries, you might have overlooked small countries. There are small countries, in which propaganda is little, military activities are almost non-existent, and people live noble lives. Unless large predator countries interfere with their politics, these small countries stay away from evil. I was privileged to visit such countries and learned much - these were Baltic Countries: Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia. These don't have military ambitions since they are located in a neutral zone between the East and the West. Other great examples are Hungary, Czech Republic and Slovenia. Most admirable is the Czech Republic for its peaceful and joyful politics and wise collective choices of its people. These countries give us much hope that the whole world will be as peaceful, wise and loving as they are.
