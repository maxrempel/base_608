# Welcome to Earth! A Guide for Aliens - THEATER

*From "Welcome to Earth! A Guide for Aliens" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Another interesting performance art is theater. Good actors transform into their characters for the time of the performance and feel the emotions of the fictional characters making them real. If you are telepathic, you may find human theater more acceptable than real human emotions, since real human emotions may be too rough for you.
