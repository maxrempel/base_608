# Metaphysics for Lightworkers - GIVING THANKS

*From "Metaphysics for Lightworkers" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

It is a very good practice to give thanks to higher energies both during and after any healing. The state of gratitude is very healing for both the healer and for the patient. At the end of the session you may say: " I thank higher energies for the healing". When the patient thanks you, thank them for the opportunity to be of service.
