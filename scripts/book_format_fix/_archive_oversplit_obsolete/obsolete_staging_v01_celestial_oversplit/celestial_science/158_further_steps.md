# Celestial Science - Further steps

*From "Celestial Science" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

The life review allows the Soul integrate the life experience with the unlimited knowledge that it can access in the spiritual plane. After the life review, the Soul is said to go to rehabilitation to heal the wounds of life. This rehabilitation process is mentioned in various sources. After rehabilitation the souls choose to get involved into service, teaching and further learning. Contrary to common misconception it is not these souls that reincarnate again it is their Higher Self entity that goes into further or parallel incarnations. The discarnate souls remain in the shape of their former incarnations forever.
