# Celestial Science - Dimension of the World of Dead

*From "Celestial Science" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

It is not clear what is the dimensional location for the World of Dead. Cassiopaeans say it is 5th density, certainly not 4th, where the beings are still largely physicalized. Traditions that study life after death assign a physical location to the world of dead to the different levels in the Earth's atmosphere and somewhat above. I am not aware of any confirmations to these claims.
