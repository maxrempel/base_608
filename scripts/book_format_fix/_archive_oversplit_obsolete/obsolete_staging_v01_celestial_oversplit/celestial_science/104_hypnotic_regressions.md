# Celestial Science - Hypnotic regressions

*From "Celestial Science" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Another way of peeking at past lives is hypnotic regression. In a session, a hypnotist assists an individual in getting into a hypnotized state (which may vary from fully conscious to fully unconscious) and the question - answer session is recorded, so you can review your answers at later time. In hypnotic regressions you don't rely on the medium it is you, your subconscious mind tells you about your past lives.
Much is known about past lives and life between lives.
Number of past lives varies. Some are discovering their past lives in Atlantean and Lemurian times. There is no restriction on gender of incarnations - individuals incarnate as both males and females. There doesn't seem to be much restriction on racial identity in incarnations: incarnations for each individual jump all over the planet and races without much order or affinity.
Surprisingly, there seems to be some affinity to the planet, although we are told that in recent times many extraterrestrials incarnate on Earth to experience and to be of service in the coming Shift. Also we are told that currently incarnated is the majority of souls of our Creator Gods, the Annunaki, Pleiadian, Sirian, Vegan and Lyran settlers and refugees who blended genetically with our ancestors in the distant past.
