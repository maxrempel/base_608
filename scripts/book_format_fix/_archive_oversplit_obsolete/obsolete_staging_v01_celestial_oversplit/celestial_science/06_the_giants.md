The angels like Archangel Michael, Archangel Metatron, are non-corporeal as well as Ascended Masters, Ra and others. Therefore main quality of the aliens and angels, is that they are from higher realms, that is heavenly, or celestial. Therefore I use the term "celestials" throughout the book and have "celestial science" for the title, signifying the unified blend of ancient and new teachings from Bashar and other channels, which I also refer to as "the New Paradigm".
