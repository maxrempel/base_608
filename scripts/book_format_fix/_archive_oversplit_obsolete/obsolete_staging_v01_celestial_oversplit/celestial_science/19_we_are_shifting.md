# Celestial Science - We are shifting

*From "Celestial Science" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

What is ahead? Naturally, this is a most important question and it also appears most controversial. We will address it based on the new knowledge obtained from celestial communications (The New Paradigm). The absolute majority of sources promise a radical transformation of the Humankind. The evolutionary Shift to a new density (from 3rd to 4th) or a new dimension (from 3rd to 5th) is the main focus of many extraterrestrial sources46,47,48,49,50. While there is agreement between the sources on the fact of the Shift in general, the timeline for the changes varies radically.
