"In Sufism what Bashar calls the Higher Self I believe is the same as the concept of the True Self, or at least a part of it. The Sufic idea is for the physical mind and the higher mind to sync absolutely and this is then the True Self.
Yet, before this unifying happens, the True Self is somewhat disconnected and occupies a similar place as the Higher Mind.
