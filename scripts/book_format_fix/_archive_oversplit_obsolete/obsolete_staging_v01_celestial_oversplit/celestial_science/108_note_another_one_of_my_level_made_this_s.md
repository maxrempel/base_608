# Celestial Science - Note: Another one of my level made this statement about incubation

*From "Celestial Science" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

"I see an egg - shaped mass with energy flowing out and back in. When it expands, new soul energy fragments are spawned. When the bulge contracts, I think it pulls back those souls which were not successfully spawned. For some reason these fragments could not make it on to the next step of individuality."
Dr. N: What do you see beyond the mass, Seena?
S: (long pause) I see this beatific glow of orange - yellow. There is a violet darkness beyond, but not cold darkness ... it is eternity.
