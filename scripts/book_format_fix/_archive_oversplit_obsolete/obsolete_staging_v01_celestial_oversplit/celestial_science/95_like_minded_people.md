# Celestial Science - Like-minded people

*From "Celestial Science" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Q: Where can we find these open-minded people?
A: The first place is the Internet. YouTube is the primary source of New Age thought these days. Lightworkers.com is great. Meetup.com is a place where anyone can find like-minded people. Local psychic fairs and psychic stores are centers where New Age folks normally center around. Theosophical Society has regular meetings in large cities. UFO interest groups may be a good resource. "Coast to Coast" radio and "virtual light internet television" are giving a general overview of the public life of the free-minded, free-thinking people who are "in the know" of the celestial knowledge, of messages from Beyond The Veil, of The New Paradigm.
