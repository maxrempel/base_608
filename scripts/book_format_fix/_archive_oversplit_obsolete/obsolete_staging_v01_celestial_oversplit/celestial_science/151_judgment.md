# Celestial Science - Judgment

*From "Celestial Science" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Lets look now at the idea of Judgment. The idea of Judgment is dominant only in Christianity. It is present in Judaism, but in Judaism it is the return to Jerusalem, rebuilding of the Temple and coming of the Messiah are the main theme. The idea of Judgment is less pronounced in Judaism. In the new Paradigm, the idea of Judgment is replaced by two very different concepts.
