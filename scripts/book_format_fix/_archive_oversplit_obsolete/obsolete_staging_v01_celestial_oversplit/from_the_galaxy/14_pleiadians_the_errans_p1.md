# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - PLEIADIANS - THE ERRANS (Part 1 of 2)

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

The civilization on the Pleiadian star Taygeta is likely closest to the Earth in terms of appearance and culture. The main planet for this civilization is called Erra, and it is the third planet of the star Taygeta. It is not a coincidence that the name Erra is similar to Terra - one of the names for Earth. Our planets are sisters in terms of culture, divine path and divine origins. Erra is populated by several alien races and Earth humans as well; some Earth humans migrated to Erra in the distant past, some migrated by fleeing the Destruction of Atlantis and some migrated from Earth in the times of Roman Empire. Erra is involved in many galactic activities, and it also hosts settlements of Earth-human-Pleiadian hybrid people.

The Errans are technologically, spiritually and culturally advanced. Modern Pleiadian culture is different from Orion culture, and the Earth is gradually moving from the Orion model to the Pleiadian model of culture. The Orion model is based on the ideas of empire, hierarchy, control, domination and wars. These ideas were brought to the Earth by Aryans from Orion in the distant past. Since then, the Pleiadians have developed the ideas of democracy, equality and compassion. The Pleiadian society is more horizontal, less structured and in many ways, resembles a tribal society structure. You can see now that the ideas of equality and non-hierarchical communes are gradually becoming more popular on Earth.

Earth humans who have visited Pleiades say that the Pleiadian culture in many ways and in many respects resembles the cultures of the tribal aliens in the movie "Avatar." They have great respect for nature, and although they have advanced technologies, they build their lives in harmony with nature. Many Errans incarnate on Earth as humans, and many Pleiadians work in our solar system to protect the solar system from invasion and help humanity to evolve and ascend.

Many Errans look like humans and could pass as humans on Earth. They are usually taller than humans, but they have a technology that allows them to reduce their height quickly. Most aliens of the fourth dimension live longer lives, between 300 and a 1,000 years. Errans have technologies to modify their body in many ways, such as to make their bodies yunger, to heal and to transform the color of their skin, hair and appearance. It is achieved quickly, comfortably and reversibly.

Errans have families and their children are brought up in families and in a community. One of the advantages of the 4D is that the people are psychic and it is easier for them to reconnect with their past life identity, skills and experiences.

Like humans, Pleiadians enjoy leisure time. They have celebrations, holidays and games. Since they are telepathic, deception is not a big part of their culture; therefore, there is much more sincerity and respect among them. When I watched the movie "La Belle Verte," I cried with tears of remembrance - this is a wonderful introduction to the Erran culture.

Errans have a government headed by a king, which is very much like a president. The Erran king's name is Kenjin. He has Earth-human genetics and looks like a human. In the past three years, Kenjin has spoken to us often through Jim and other channelers. Kenjin consults the Erran elders on many questions of Erran government, and the involvement of government in the life of the Errans is much lighter than it is on Earth.

Erran culture is open to the galaxy and Erra has many visitors from other parts of the galaxy. The Erran culture spreads far beyond the planet Erra; it is a unified, tightly connected culture because they have a way of quick travel and communication through their technology and telepathy. The Erran culture and people are closely connected to certain locations in the Andromeda galaxy, likely via a system of wormholes.

Pleiadians have been involved in the hybridization with humans, and many human hybrids exist in Pleiadian planets and space settlements. Pleiadian-human hybrids develop their own culture based on Erran culture. They study the culture of Earth and are ready to visit when invited by the Earth humans. They are eager to help us by consulting with us about possible solutions to our challenges and about our entrance into the galactic community.

Some Pleiadians look like Scandinavians. Others have brown or black skin and look like Central Africans. There is great variety in their appearance, even more than among humans. Since ecology is important to Pleiadians, they have developed a technology that allows them to absorb sunlight to generate energy in their bodies via photosynthesis of glucose. This technology adds chlorophyll in their skin, which makes the skin become green and blue.

Pleiadians can modify the body through genetics and transdimensional manipulation. Their surgery is advanced. They can access inner parts of the body trans-dimensionally without damaging surrounding tissues. They can also manipulate time, too, for medical purposes: they can slow down time in the body during the surgery and speed up time in the body after the medical treatments to speed up regeneration.

Although Pleiadian medicine is much more advanced than ours, there are still some similarities. In addition to bioenergetic, dimensional and genetic medicine, they use advanced pharmacology. This is somewhat unexpected, since we, the lightworkers of Earth, have lost trust in chemical pharmacology and  prefer herbal, naturopathic and energetic medicine over drugs. Yet Pleiadian medicine uses pharmacology, among other means.

Many children on Pleiades choose to speed up their growth using time manipulation technology. Many  choose to speed up their studies, too. This is considered normal, since there are no side effects and they still live long lives. This accelerated childhood can take much less time than on Earth: years of studies can be jammed in a few months and the child becomes a grownup packed with knowledge. The areas of study are chosen by the children with the help of advisers. This accelerated childhood makes Pleiadians quite different from Earth humans; they miss the experience of childhood and the jump into adulthood without experiencing dependence, protected play, childhood limitations, restrictions, etc. Therefore, the hybrid children who intend to learn about humans and work in the future with humans sometimes choose to experience slow growth and learning in the childhood. They learn our games and children's culture.

On the other hand, our childhood on Earth is almost always loaded with repression of the child, child abuse, psychological trauma and violence. Even if the parents are enlightened, there is plenty of repression and violence in school. Pleiadians have no experience of that, and this makes them even more harmonized and uninhibited.
