# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - PLEIADIANS: THE MAYANS

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

The Mayan civilization on Earth has come from the Maya star in the Pleiades. The Mayans visited Earth in ancient times and thrived on Earth, before returning back to Maya. It is not clear why the Mayan civilization failed and why they moved back to the Pleiades, although it was likely due to a moral failure and the use of dark magic. The main Mayan civilization on the Maya star is now very advanced, spiritual and enlightened. In recent years, they have started to channel to us  wonderful, enlightening messages.
