# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - THE HYBRIDIZATION PROGRAM (Part 2 of 3)

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

The process of hybridization involves several steps where humans are needed:  First, it is necessary to obtain their genetic material - sperm cells from men and egg cells (ova) from women. Next, even with the alien technologies, it is difficult to grow human fetuses in incubators. Human fetuses require mother's energies and nutrients especially at early stages of development. So women are taken to the ships, impregnated with hybrid embryos and returned to Earth. Since the impregnation is done transdimensionally via teleportation, this should be considered as immaculate conception. For the hybrid children which are born on Earth, the aliens usually make the hybrid child from the genes of both parents and add alien DNA. If the child is to live off-planet, the embryo is allowed to grow for a few weeks before being taken away. To be impregnated in an alien or hybrid mother who would later give birth. Alternatively, some hybrid embryos are grown in incubators. These cases of of women losing an embryo at early stage of development are usually interpreted as miscarriage. Yet, these cases are reminiscent of the fairytale of Rumpelstiltskin. And yet, for some people, donating an early stage embryo to the hybridization program could be a conscious voluntary action. The reasons for such a donation could be rather practical: (1) The parents might not have material resources to raise the child on Earth; (2) Giving the child an opportunity to live in a 4D reality, with advanced technology and freedom of space travel; (3) Seeding their genes to other stars; (4) Having a relative in the free galactic world could entitle the parents for the right to visit their child - as it is with the parents who obtain the right to immigrate to the United States to rejoin their children. The 4th consideration, although selfish could become quite practical especially in the later years when Earth is predicted to become a member of the Galaxy.

The third place where humans participate in the hybridization program, is in helping to nurture the hybrid children who remain off-planet. The children born in alien space feel out of place, so the aliens would bring human volunteers to serve as babysitters and to play with the children and help educate them. In this way, the children would feel the human warmth and become healthier and  more accustomed to earth-human's traditions.

The memory of the majority of the abductees were manipulated so that they would not remember the abductions. However, after some years, the memories start to resurface and abductees recollect their abductions. This information is valuable for the abductees and the humanity since these are glimpses into the alien worlds.

The hybridization program by the friendly Yahyel and Pleiadians continues to this day. And now, this hybridization program only utilizes human volunteers who give a conscious consent for the participation in the program. The hybrids live in the alien space and the are full fledged members of the society with full rights. They're free to move around and  have free access to alien information.

These hybrids grow up in the Pleiadian or Yahyel cultures,  but they also choose to learn the Earth culture and are eager to visit the Earth when they're permitted to visit and help as advisers. Their scientific and practical knowledge of the galactic life is very valuable. Some of the hybrid people are physiologically capable of living on earth for long periods of time.

The aliens that work with humans in the hybridization program have developed an understanding of the humans and compassion. They have even modified themselves to experience human emotions  so they could understand human psychology.

By now, the hybridization procedure and technology has much improved and humanized: the program takes only volunteers, the volunteers are treated kindly, the procedures are comfortable, the failures and complications happen less frequently than in human medicine.  The hybrids are  healthy and have rich and creative lives.

As i mentioned, one of the positive outcomes from the hybridization program is that Earth human genes become spreaded over the universe. Yet, surprisingly, many descendants of Earth humans live in our and Andromeda galaxies. For example, before the destructions of Atlantis, many Atlanteans escaped to the Pleiades and to the Andromeda. Another group of humans was taken as slaves from the ancient Roman Empire by the Pleiadians to the planet Erra. Since that time, the slaves were liberated, emancipated and became citizens of Erra with full rights. Even Kenjin, the current King-president of Erra, is genetically an Earth human.

It is remarkable that natural Earth humans with unaltered earthly genetics can live comfortably in the 4th density, they are fully telepathic and fully capable of being citizens of the alien worlds and are treated as equals. This means that biologically, we are ready for Ascension and for traveling into the Galaxy, it is only our politics that prevents us from awakening, Ascension and entering the Galaxy

One of the positive outcomes of the hybridization programs is the appearance on earth of starseeds, indigo children and crystal children. 'Starseeds' are humans who have been seeded from the stars. These would usually be the those who have partially alien genetics, but also, we often have alien past lives. So, we are often linked to the stars not only genetically, but also on the soul level, incarnationally.

'Indigo children' are usually defined as those whose aura is of indigo color, which corresponds to high level of spiritual development, connection to higher densities of the spirit worlds, higher development of the upper chakras of the body and higher psychic abilities.

'Crystal children' is another name for the newly appearing generation of humans who have an advanced design of the spiritual body, the chakras, and advanced psychic abilities. The world 'crystal' symbolizes the idea of purity and harmony.

While it is often said that indigo children are not compliant and have trouble fitting in with society, 'crystal children' are known to be good at socializing and are fully engaged in social life. The indigo children could behave like revolutionaries and troublemakers, while crystal children would change reality through peaceful evolutionary ways.

Although slightly different, these terms  (starseeds, crystal children and indigo children) are often used interchangeably. They indicate an idea that humanity is currently evolving into a new species, which has been prophesied throughout  all our history. One of the clearest prophecies has been given by Blavatsky 126 years ago in a book The Secret Doctrine published in 1881. There, she clearly outlines the Lemurian race, the Atlantean race, and the  extraterrestrial involvement in the development new genetic design of earth human via hybridization which she called 'the 6th race'.
