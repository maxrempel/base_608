# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - THE YAHYEL

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

The Yahyel are an advanced extraterrestrial race friendly to humanity, and a great many of them live in the Pleiades, being part of the Pleiadian culture. Among the galactic races, the Yahyel are the closest to humans in their DNA sequence, which is over 95% identical to humans. The appearances of the Yahyel are very diverse. While some look very much like humans and would pass as humans on Earth's streets, others look like Tall Greys with very light skin. There are also many Yahyel who are in between, looking partly human and partly Grey. As it is with the origins of Earth humans, there are several conflicting stories about the origins of the Yahyel. Bashar says that the Yahyel are one of the hybrid races that have been created by the Zeta Greys, and the Yahyel are a hybrid of the Zetas and Earth humans. However, Pentim, a Yahyel channeled by Jim Charles, says that the Yahyel are not descendants of the Zetas. It is also possible that the Yahyel and the Zetas have a common ancestor with the Zetas, which would solve the contradiction.

The Yahyel, as well as other extraterrestrials, have been visiting Earth since ancient times. In general, different human races have been seeded as hybrids by different extraterrestrials. It is likely that the ancient Jews, Arabs and Assyrians were seeded on Earth as Yahyel-human hybrids by the Yahyel.

Psychologically, the Yahyel are less emotional than humans. The range of their emotions is narrower and they have fewer types of emotions. Their strongest Yahyel emotion is their kindness and caring. They are the species that cares and seeds life across the Universe. That is their calling and passion. They're compassionate and non-aggressive.

While many Yahyel live on planets in the Pleiades cluster, many others live on ships traveling across the Galaxy. Yahyel physiology and their ships have been adapted for the long-term life in space. Few of the Yahyel procreate like humans. Most Yahyel multiply technologically, and their fetuses are grown in incubators. The majority of Yahyel don't have families and they grow up in social groups similar in principle to our day care, kindergartens and schools.

Yahyel have exceptional telepathic abilities. Unlike Zetas, who are plugged into a collective hive mind, each Yahyel individual has an independent, individual mind, though it is likely that Yahel individuals may connect to their collective mind as we do by connecting to our internet.

The Yahyel have very strong intellectual abilities. They live long lives (over 700 human years), and when they reincarnate, they can develop spiritually and recall their past lives. The Yahyel are very spiritual and have strong psychic abilities. They also have strong telekinetic abilities, a physiological ability of mind control (even without the use of technology), and a natural ability to bi-locate. It is normal for them to be actively working in many places at the same time. Their way of transportation is just to appear and disappear at different locations or to phase-shift from one location to another. They are capable of doing this physiologically, even without the use of technology. They also have a good understanding of human medicine and are strong healers and doctors.

Since the Yahyel are genetically very similar to humans, and since many of them look like humans, they have been designated by several other extraterrestrial races as the first race to establish Open Contact with humanity.

The Yahyel are a part of the Girk-Fitneer alliance (Girk-Fitneer alliance is an alliance of extraterrestrial-friendly races, consisting of  Yahyel, Lyrans, Pleiadians, Arcturians and friendly Reptilians, the Lashunda and Fendorians). Yahyel, as a part of Girk-Fitneer, are hosting a voluntary hybridization program and settlements of Yahyel-Human and Pleiadian-Human hybrids. The hybridization program is abiding by volunteering principles, accepting genetic material only from Earth humans who provide informed consent.

On March 13, 1997 Yahyel ships openly passed over the city of Phoenix, Arizona to attract public attention, but this information was repressed by the media. This event is known as the Phoenix Lights. Currently, the Yahyel, as a part of the Girk-Fitneer Alliance, are negotiating with major human governments. They promote the idea of the Disclosure and Open Contact and offer to consult these humans on important questions. Unfortunately, so far it has been the choice of the human governments to keep these contacts secret.

Some of the humans on Earth carry ancient Yahyel genes, and some of the humans are recently emerged Yahyel hybrids. The qualities of the Yahyel hybrids are: expanded intellectual abilities, reduced emotional range, limited ability for anger, expanded ability for kindness, expanded psychic and telepathic abilities, an ability to empathically link with other Yahyel hybrids, healing abilities and the ability to influence the minds of other people telepathically.
