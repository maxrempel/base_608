# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - OPEN CONTACT

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

The key step in the collective preparation for the Ascension is Open Contact with the aliens. In fact, the contact with the aliens happens daily on Earth but it is not a part of global public awareness. It is the global Open Contact which hasn't happened yet. Individual humans and groups are in contact with the aliens daily. There are many forms of contact: some people are visited, some are taken to the ships and stars, some communicate telepathically and many speak to aliens via channelers. Some aliens live on earth and even have families with humans and hybrids. Each year, the public is getting closer to realizing that contact is possible and at some point will be ready to invite the aliens to speak to us openly. The friendly aliens are eager to speak to us but they are limited by a so-called First Directive and can not show up until invited by the human collective.

Many channelled communications predict that Open Contact will happen soon, most likely within 10 years (before 2027). It is determined by the collective awareness and by the collective desire of the people of the planet. Currently, we are represented by politicians who find it safe and profitable to delay the Open Contact as long as possible.

The top levels of Earth governments are aware of the aliens and have regular meetings with them but yet are afraid of disclosing these connections to the public because of the fear of losing control. If the public learns about the aliens and their technologies, they would not be willing to support the ongoing wars, the armies and the military industry. The politicians realize that Open Contact would likely cause the disclosure of many shameful secrets and result in the redistribution of power on the planet. Nonetheless, the process of Ascension has already begun and it is absolutely essential for the population to become aware of it. Delays in the Ascension process would be detrimental to our civilization. Since meeting the aliens face to face would greatly help the collective awareness, we, the lightworkers of Earth, advocate for the Open Contact to be organized as soon as possible.

We advocate for disclosure in which the governments shall release to the public the information about their interactions with the aliens. We advocate for the Open Contact in which the public and the governments invite the aliens to come down and openly begin their consultations with the public and the officials. Until that happens, we continue to communicate with the aliens on an individual level and share our experiences online.
