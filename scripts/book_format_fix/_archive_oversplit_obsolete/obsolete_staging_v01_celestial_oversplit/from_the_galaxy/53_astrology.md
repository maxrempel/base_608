# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - ASTROLOGY

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Birth is a very significant moment in life. Even before the birth, the child is partly conscious, but the passage via the birth canal and the first breath are essential. With breath, a new part of the soul connects to the body. During birth, the child's brain is reprogrammed from a passive to an active program. At this moment, the pattern of the electromagnetic waves of the planet is imprinted on the brain and the body of the child. This pattern of the planet's electromagnetic field at the time of birth is defined by the relative position of the child, the planet, the sun and other planets in the solar system. This is known as the birth chart in astrology.

The interaction of the wave imprint on the person's physical and energetic bodies at the time of birth with the planet's electromagnetic field during the person's life is the physical basis of astrology.

Interestingly, when the birth is not stimulated, it is the fetus that defines the hour of birth. At the right time, the fetus releases stimulants into the bloodstream of the mother, signaling the start of labor. Even more fascinating is that the time of birth is an inheritable trait, and that is written in the child's DNA.
