# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - ABOUT YAHYEL BY DISDOO

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Channeled by Jim

Disdoo is from Yahyel race. His full name is Disyakabudisduda. He is a captain of a scientific research ship of Girk Fitneer working on stabilising our planet's climate and prevent disasters such as axis flip,  major earthquakes or oceanic stream reversal.

Disdoo: Welcome everyone. I am Disdoo from the vessel over North America.

Max: How old are you?

Disdoo: In Earth years? 137.

Max: So you are very young?

Disdoo: Yes.

Max: I think you live about 500 to 600 years?

Disdoo: Yes. But I moved quickly up the ranks because of my ability to communicate.

Max: Back in the 1940s, the Roswell incident in New Mexico? Can you tell us a little about that?

Disdoo: I can tell you a little but not a lot because your government knows everything about that and they were Yahyel in that accident. They were Greys  and they were friendly.

Max:  Were they Greys or Yahyel?

Disdoo: Greys are Yahyel but a different species than Yahyel. Yet they look very much the same.  There are five different Grey species.

Max:  Earlier you talked about abductions? There are no more forced ones, as  that was a while back. Yet can one still be abducted in the dream state?

Disdoo: Yes, but our species and the species that we are in alliance with do not do any form of abductions anymore. There are species that still do abductions but we are not of that species or alliance. However, yes, they still do that.

Max: Can you tell me about your religion and your connection to God and religious practices?

Disdoo: We do much meditation when we can, but even our work is our religion because we are helping move people up into a greater vibration. We are working at a spirit level with humanity.

We are also working in a physical, natural level but our intention started when it was spirit.

Therefore, our religion is helping you, if perhaps you needed help, which you do at this point .

Yet, you help us as well with your prayers, thoughts and positive actions, as well as encourage us to do our job better.

Max: Are you praying using words? Are you in contact with the Angels and maybe some higher consciousness which you also know?

Disdoo: Angels choose when they want to come into your reality. It is the same with us. If they want to be in our reality, they make themselves available. They give us the message and they let us know what they want us to know.

Max: Do we know or have something in common with your Gods or your higher consciousness? Perhaps we also know their names? You are in connection with them, and praise them?

Disdoo: Oh,  yes. God would be your name and Allah is known in the Universe. Yes, we pray to God,  Allah and Ra.

Max: Is Ra a separate being than Allah?

Disdoo: Yes, but slightly underneath, that's all.

Max: Are you in connection with Ra?

Disdoo: Correct

Max: Ra Material is very much correct, right? ["Ra material" was channeled by Carla Rueckert in 60s]

Disdoo: Yes.
