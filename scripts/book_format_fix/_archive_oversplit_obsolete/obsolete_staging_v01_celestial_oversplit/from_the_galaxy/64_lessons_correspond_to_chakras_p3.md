# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - LESSONS CORRESPOND TO CHAKRAS (Part 3 of 3)

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Another resonance is between chakras 3 and 6, solar plexus, will power, hierarchy (3) and mind (6). Understand, that for many, graduation into the 3rd chakra is an achievement. This is the main achievement of the humankind of the past to develop the will power, responsibility and  sense duty. 3rd chakra is a chakra of a manager and of a warrior. People with the strong 3rd chakra think fast, are grounded and make fewer routine mistakes, that is required of a warrior and a manager. Their talent is to submit others to their will power. They can be decent leaders. Yet, to become true leaders, they need to develop visionary qualities, which are in the 6th chakra. That requires shifting the vibration 3 levels up. The worrieriors attempt achieving this with their willpower, but the 6th chakra is not about heat, it is about cool, inspirational thought and vision. So it takes art and spiritual growth to develop the 6th chakra's inspirational vision. So it is a challenge and an art to shift from anger, fear and competition-driven mindset (3) to inspirational thinking, vision, unity and harmony. This is achieved through a consistent development and harmonization of all higher chakras heart (4), throat (5), third eye (6) and crown (7).
