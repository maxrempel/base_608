# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - THE ALIENS ARE FROM ANOTHER DIMENSION

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

This book is intended as a textbook about friendly aliens and Ascension. Although the subjects are complex and dramatic, I will keep them simple and focus on the positive side.

This book is the result of the research I have done for the last seven years. The information is up-to-date as of early 2017. I think that the emergence of aliens and hybrid children is the most important event that is happening to humanity. We have great hopes for the future of humanity as it awakens to the visitation by aliens and is therefore transformed in the process.

The first source of information about the aliens is experiencers. Experiencers are humans who have met the aliens face to face, spoken to them directly and visited their ships and planets. Another source of information is channeling. In a channeling session, a channeler enters a trance state, connects telepathically to an alien and thus mediates the dialogue between the audience and the aliens. This allows the audience to speak with the aliens, as well as ask questions and get answers from them.

This book is based on the channelings of James Ernest Charles, the co-author of this book, as well as other channelers listed in the Acknowledgements section at the end of the book. Here I will summarize what I learned and illustrate the main points with selected quotations from the channeled sessions.

Most of the aliens visiting Earth and interacting with humans are from other dimensions. That means they exist in a different reality and are invisible to us, yet they have the technology to materialize in our reality and be visible and material. Many of them can exist on our planet for only a few hours before they have to return to their dimension. They can also take humans to their worlds in their ships. Many human experiencers have visited their worlds, where they can usually stay for only as long as two weeks; if they stay longer, their physiology adapts to the alien reality, and it is hard for them to readapt to our reality after they return.

The world from which the aliens come is usually called the 4th Density, 4th Dimension or even the 5th Dimension when a different numbering system is used. The aliens that speak through Jim (the co-author of this book) call our reality 3D and their reality 4D, so we will use this numbering throughout.

The experiencers that visit 4D have mentioned that they feel much healthier on alien ships, and their illnesses go away until the time they come back to our reality. Life in 4D is different from ours. The most notable difference in 4D is telepathy: 4D beings are telepathic, many having psychic and telekinetic abilities that allow them to talk with spirits and manipulate matter with their mind. The main perception of 4D is that it is lighter than our 3D. Even time in 4D is more flexible; some 4D aliens have the ability to shift back and forth in time by a few hours. In our 3D, we only move forward in time, whereas the inhabitants of 4D possess the ability to shift their consciousness back and forth and clairvoyantly see some of the future.
