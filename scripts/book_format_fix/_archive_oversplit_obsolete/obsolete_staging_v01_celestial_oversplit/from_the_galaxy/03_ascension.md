# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - ASCENSION

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

Ascension is the process of shifting to a higher density and dimension. This is a way many civilizations in the universe shifted from 3D to 4D. Humanity has started the Ascension, too. Currently, it is at an early stage: only a few people - mostly from aboriginal cultures - have ascended individually. The timing of the collective Ascension is uncertain, as it is dependent on our collective awakening. If the awakening of humanity continues to progress slowly, the massive Ascension is predicted to happen only about 180 years from now (around the year 2300), which would be our great-grandchildren's generation. However, there is much hope that humanity's collective awakening will be sped up by the Open Contact with friendly aliens. Then humanity could possibly ascend in our lifetime. Currently, the 4D Earth (Tara Ha) is sparsely populated; Its whole population is only about one million people, the majority being aliens from the Pleiadian and Yahyel cultures. Some Reptilians and some ascended humans are also there.

It is predicted that not all humans will ascend, however. Ascension is a conscious process, and an adult person must be willing to ascend. Some people will ascend individually and some will ascend in families and other groups. The main requirement for Ascension is the desire to ascend and a certain level of spiritual purity, which can be attained through meditation and positive intentions. It is predicted that less than fifty percent of the human population will ascend, with the rest remaining on the 3D Earth.

For each individual, the Ascension process takes about a week on 3D Earth, during which the physical body transforms to a light body. That process can be helped by meditation and prayer. It is a conscious process of transforming and transmuting your body. As a result, the body disappears from the 3D Earth and shifts to the 4D Earth. ,

It's a big decision to leave this reality and shift to a new reality, and there is work that needs to be done to bring and disseminate information about the Ascension. Work is also needed on a spiritual level to help the human collective to accept the idea of Ascension and to commit to it. In this learning and collective acceptance, we receive help from aliens, angels and spirits. The information comes in the form of channeling, dream work and direct contacts. Once the contact with the aliens becomes open, the process of learning and collective awakening will speed up. Once the human collective awakens to the idea of Ascension, there will be much discussion, and the communication with the aliens will expand, so by the time of the wave of Ascension, people will know what to expect.

Upon the arrival to the 4D Earth, people will have much assistance. Their physiology will accommodate to the 4D reality in about a month. Here, a person goes into Ascension by themselves, but on the other side there is much assistance with accommodation. Children will ascend together with their parents and pets with their owners.

Physiologically, Ascension involves the activation of the DNA, of the nervous system and of the energetics of the body, which is similar to the Kundalini awakening. People of aboriginal cultures who reach spiritual maturity are currently ascending at a slow rate. A very tiny percentage of Westerners ascend, those who are in a positive state of mind and health, and who are consciously working on ascension. The current rate of ascension is very low, as only a few hundred people per year ascend. Currently, the majority of the ascended humans are accommodated by the existing alien population on the 4D Earth. As the rate of ascension increases, the newly ascended humans will start developing their own culture.

The 4D reality is not new to humans. The Earth has been in 4D before. It was a great catastrophe a long time ago that caused the descension of the planet from 4D to 3D. This fall of humanity is remembered in many ancient traditions, including the Bible. According to Ashayana Deane and David Wilcock, the fall from 4D happened about 500 million years ago. After that, the human civilization was rebuilt several times and  destroyed several times. According to Bashar and other sources, the last and major destruction of Atlantis happened about 23,000 years ago. This fall caused a loss of culture and technology. Some of the Atlantean knowledge has been kept by secret societies and priests, and now some of it is resurfacing again.

Among major advantages of 4D life is that it is rich in spirit and there are better connections among people due to telepathic communication. 4D life is much lighter and happier, and the lessons are of a higher vibrational nature. Of course, there is drama, challenges and lessons in the 4D, but these are lessons of a higher level. Ascension is like graduating from middle school to high school; the lessons become more complex and interesting.

The idea of the past downfall and approaching Ascension is central to human culture and has been mentioned throughout many religions and spiritual texts from around the world. In addition, the Ascension was predicted by many prophets, including Jesus (Yeshua). Ascension is a central idea in many current channelings and teachings. Much work is needed from us to help humanity awaken and prepare for Ascension, and much work is being done by our higher-dimensional friends from 4D and higher spirit dimensions to help the Ascension. It is a process that is guided by the Creator.

To illustrate the idea of Ascension, consider a computer analogy: Ascension could be compared to a major upgrade of the operating system. Also consider a computer game analogy: Ascension would correspond to reaching a new level in the game, where each new level gives you new powers and new challenges.

Currently, only a small fraction of society is aware of ascension, and even a smaller number of people have a desire to Ascend. Many humans are stuck in the 3D drama and are afraid of the unknown. Even the idea of aliens and spirits frightens many people. For humanity to ascend, a major awakening will be required.
