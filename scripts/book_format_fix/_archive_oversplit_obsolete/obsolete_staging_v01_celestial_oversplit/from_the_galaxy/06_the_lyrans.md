# From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook - THE LYRANS

*From "From the Galaxy, With Love: A Lightworker's Textbook" by Max Rempel, Ph.D.*

The first galactic humans that appeared in our Milky Way galaxy were Lyrans. They are referred to as Founders. Until the arrival of Lyrans, the galaxy was populated by Reptilians and Insectoids. The Lyrans came from outside of this galaxy and settled on a planet called Lyra, which means "rock" or "soil" in the Lyran language. The ancient Lyran home star is not located in our constellation that we call Lyra and was in a different sector of our sky, northeast of Alpha Centauri, at a distance far beyond Alpha Centauri.

The Lyrans developed a peaceful civilization and advanced culturally, technologically and spiritually until a tragic day when their planet, Lyra, was destroyed by the Reptilians. The Lyrans knew in advance that the Reptilian ships were coming, evacuated much of the population before its destruction and took many treasures of their culture with them into space. Plant life, animals, books, technologies and even pieces of buildings were taken with them as they scattered throughout the galaxy. This diaspora of refugees created several new subspecies and cultures of Lyrans as they continued to evolve on other planets. This is how galactic humans appeared and spread over our galaxy.

The Lyrans cherish their ancient culture and language. An average Lyran is about 9 feet tall. They are feline humans; like lions in a tall, humanoid body. Their bone structure and other features are more feline, but the overall shape of the body is like that of an Earth human. Lyrans are very strong, proud and kind. They are our ancestors and friends of our civilization.

Many Lyrans are incarnated as Earth humans and some humans carry ancient Lyran bloodlines in their DNA. The presence of Lyran DNA in a human gives the person strength, emotional power, telepathic and psychic abilities. To some humans, Lyrans serve as spirit guides.

Biologically, the Lyrans are placentals and mammals as we are. They give birth to their children and feed them with milk. The mammaries of Lyrans are much smaller than of humans and lessen in size when not in use (as in lions). The faces of Lyrans are covered with hair. Lyran women have longer beards covering their faces, as we see in lions. The Lyrans have low voices, with the females having lower voices than males.

The Lyrans do not find humans to be sexually attractive, and humans would not find Lyrans to be sexually attractive, either. Otherwise, we are sufficiently similar, so that the Lyrans and the humans can become friends. Lyrans and humans will easily hug, but before hugging a Lyran, ask them to be be gentle since they are exceptionally strong.

The Lyrans are very spiritually advanced. They are psychic and clairvoyant. Their chakra system and spiritual powers are exceptionally developed. They are powerful healers. The Lyran culture places a special value on emotional power and spiritual integrity. They are wise and serve the progress of galactic mankind across the universe.
