About the Author

Max Rempel, Ph.D., is a molecular geneticist with 4,500+ citations, h-index 21, published in Nature and Genome Research. He leads the Starseed Genetics Research Project through the DNA Resonance Research Foundation, analyzing family DNA trios to identify genetic variants in children that cannot be explained by parental inheritance. He co-founded Human Colony (Hucolo), a channeling community hosting weekly webinars since 2013. His books include "Welcome to Earth! A Guide for Aliens", "Celestial Science," and "From the Galaxy, With Love." He lives in San Diego, California.
